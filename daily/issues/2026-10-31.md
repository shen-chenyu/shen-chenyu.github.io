## Featured Papers

### [Elucidating the Design Space of LM Data Attribution](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40655v1)
**Authors:** Steven Yu, Sheng-Yu Wang, Kimia Hamidieh, Antonio Torralba, David Bau
**Published:** 2026-10-29
**Summary:** CATT benchmark isolates why LM data attribution fails, showing redundancy and distractors hurt, and negative log-odds gradients plus top-token aggregation boost recall 35-72 points.

**What's new**
- Introduces CATT, a controlled fact-tracing benchmark using fictitious knowledge for unambiguous attribution ground truth.
- Shows document redundancy and lexical distractors consistently degrade attribution performance.
- Finds true causal evidence is highly localized, but averaging scores across documents dilutes it into noise.
- Replacing cross-entropy with negative log-odds gradients surfaces critical tokens, improving recall 35-72 points.
- Negative log-odds correction generalizes to FTRACE-TREX, improving counterfactual retraining up to 4.9x.

### [ShallowDefend: Harnessing Shallow Safety Alignment for Multi-Turn Defender](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40627v1)
**Authors:** Qiyao Ma, Xiaodong Liu, Weiwei Yang, Mingqian Feng, Chengquan Guo, Jialin Song, Carolyn Buractaon, Zhe Zhao, Junshan Zhang, Sandeep Atluri, Christopher White, Jianfeng Gao
**Published:** 2026-10-29
**Summary:** ShallowDefend trains a model to commit an explicit safety decision in its first tokens, turning shallow alignment into a multi-turn defense.

**What's new**
- Proposes Assess→Decide→Respond: assess intent, commit a parseable safety label, then generate conditioned on it.
- Trains the decision commitment with RL at the payload turn using a self-verified rule reward needing no judge or annotation.
- Also supports LLM-as-judge rewards over a graded decision space with safe redirection.
- Achieves strongest multi-turn safety across two model families and fourteen benchmarks, preserving utility at modest helpfulness cost.
- KL and interventional probes show the committed decision is a causal control point, explaining the defense.

### [Generalizing Density Functional Theory with a Particle-Hole Expansion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40621v1)
**Authors:** Shurui Zhao, Madhubani Mukherjee, David A. Mazziotti
**Published:** 2026-10-29
**Summary:** A particle-hole expansion generalizes DFT via a one-electron reduced density matrix functional, with corrections that reduce excessive orbital fractionalization in strongly correlated systems.

**What's new**
- Introduces a systematic particle-hole expansion generalizing DFT through an explicit 1-RDM functional.
- Shows the cumulant-based correction is the leading term and the thermal entropic correction an infinite-order member.
- Formulates the expansion as a moment problem solvable by semidefinite programming at DFT-like scaling.
- First- and second-order corrections yield closed-form natural-orbital occupations.
- Second-order correction improves occupations and fundamental gaps for periacenes and Fe-porphyrin.

**What's new**
