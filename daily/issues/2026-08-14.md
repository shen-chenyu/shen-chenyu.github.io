### [Information Abundance Paradox: Long-Context Training Undermines Parametric Knowledge](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12218v1)
**Authors:** Arda Uzunoglu, Benjamin van Durme, Daniel Khashabi
**Published:** 2026-08-12
**Summary:** Long-context training can paradoxically reduce a model's parametric knowledge, shifting reliance to context and hurting performance when context is absent.

**What's new**
- Proposes the Information Abundance Paradox: abundant context reduces incentive to encode knowledge parametrically.
- Shows pretraining with longer contexts improves performance only up to an optimum, then declines.
- In SFT, more train-time context improves in-context performance but reduces robustness without or with misleading context.
- Mechanistically, informative context shifts gradient pressure from feed-forward networks to attention modules.
- Causal interventions confirm increased reliance on context during inference due to this shift.

**What's new**
