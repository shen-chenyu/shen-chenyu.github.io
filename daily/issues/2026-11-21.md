## Featured Papers

### [RL Post-Training in Diffusion Transformers Is Activation Steering](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26627v1)
**Authors:** Tuna Han Salih Meral, Kaan Oktay, Alec Helbling, Atakan Kara, Duen Horng Chau, Pinar Yanardag
**Published:** 2026-11-19
**Summary:** Most of the gain from RL post-training of diffusion transformers can be recovered by a fixed, input-independent set of steering vectors added to block outputs.

**What's new**
- Fixed steering vectors with 65x fewer parameters than RL-tuned LoRA recover 90.0-96.3% of reward gains on six image models.
- The vectors are identical for every prompt, token, and sampling seed, yet match RL-tuned velocity predictions without any reward in the objective.
- On two video models, the same approach recovers 89.8% and 111.9% of RL reward gains.
- Initialized at zero and trained with online RL, the vectors alone exceed weight-based RL gains on OCR and PickScore for SD3.5-M.
- Findings suggest policy-gradient RL post-training largely steers the pretrained generator toward what it can already compute rather than learning new input-dependent transformations.

### [TideCache: Efficient Hybrid LLM Serving with Transparent Cache Management and Layer-Wise Streaming](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26619v1)
**Authors:** Jinbin Luo, Xiaozhe Yao, Shang Yang, Zhijian Liu, Song Han, Qinghao Hu
**Published:** 2026-11-19
**Summary:** TideCache is a hybrid LLM serving system using transparent cache pooling and layer-wise streaming to cut GPU memory residency and boost throughput.

**What's new**
- Transparent Cache Pool redistributes physical pages behind fixed virtual addresses, preserving cache layouts and backend interfaces.
- Cache Streamer prefetches next global-attention prefix during bounded-state layer computation, overlapping transfers with compute.
- Request Scheduler picks resident or streamed execution and triggers capacity adjustments based on request size and queue state.
- Streaming frees HBM for other cache components and extra requests, improving concurrency.
- On GB300/GH200, completes up to 2.04x agent turns and cuts P50 per-token latency 20-59% vs SGLang with HiCache.

### [Fractional Charge at Dislocations from Band-Inversion Cancellation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26616v1)
**Authors:** Vladimir Juričić, Rodrigo Soto-Garrido
**Published:** 2026-11-19
**Summary:** Dislocations in inversion-symmetric two-band crystals with cancelling band inversions bind a half-integer charge, revealing quantized bulk polarization of the obstructed atomic limit.

**What's new**
- Shows dislocations bind half-integer charge in crystals with cancelling band inversions, probing topology invisible to Hall response.
- Deforms two-band models into decoupled chains without closing the gap or breaking inversion symmetry, counting occupied bonds.
- Fractional charge is robust to dislocation-core perturbations and persists after the in-gap core state is removed.
- In the Weyl-semimetal counterpart, magnetic flux separates this crystalline charge from the Chern-number charge.
- Proposes detection in artificial electronic/optical lattices and metamaterials via persistent half-integer core charge.

### [High-Accuracy Neural-Network Quantum States via Randomized Real-Time Dynamics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26587v1)
**Authors:** John M. Martyn, Rod Rofougaran, Samuel Stein, Di Luo
**Published:** 2026-11-19
**Summary:** Dynamical averaging uses randomized real-time evolution to make NQS observables converge up to quadratically, matching energy accuracy without extra optimization or model complexity.

**What's new**
- Introduces dynamical averaging: estimates expectation values by averaging over a real-time evolved state or observable.
- Proves up-to-quadratic suppression of error in arbitrary ground-state observables, closing the gap with energy convergence.
- Achieves higher accuracy without further energy minimization or increased model complexity, exploiting randomization.
- Demonstrated on non-integrable spin models, accelerating convergence and substantially reducing observable errors.

**Published:** 2026-11-19
**Summary:** EditSafetyBench reveals image editing systems are text-aware but vision-blind, refusing unsafe prompts far more than unsafe images with neutral instructions.

**What's new**

### [Budget Internalization: Fixed-Budget RLVR Trades Test-Time Scaling for In-Budget Accuracy](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26172v1)
**Authors:** Ilia Zavidnyi, Alexander Kovrigin, Arina Puchkova, Kirill Gelvan, Sergey Titov
**Published:** 2026-11-19
**Summary:** RLVR's fixed rollout cap becomes a learned constraint: models stop improving beyond the training budget, trading test-time scaling for in-budget accuracy.

**What's new**
- Identifies 'budget internalization': models trained with a rollout cap use few tokens beyond it and lose accuracy at larger budgets.
- Observed across Qwen3.5-4B, Gemma4-E2B, Nemotron-3-Nano-4B on math and procedural tasks, transferring to science and coding.
- Most training-objective ablations recover little test-time scaling; stronger KL or lower truncation preserve some but cost in-budget accuracy.
- Forcing answers on truncated rollouts removes the effect but also most in-budget gains.
- SFT on artificially truncated traces reproduces it, linking the effect to length-censored post-training, not RL-specific objectives.
