### [Cell Natural Orbitals in Interacting Topological Bands](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09932v1)
**Authors:** Nishchhal Verma, Harshitra Mahalingam, Daniel Muñoz-Segovia, Raquel Queiroz
**Published:** 2026-08-10
**Summary:** Introduces Cell Natural Orbitals (CNOs) via SVD of band-projected form factors to systematically truncate Hilbert space and capture interaction hierarchy in topological bands.

**What's new**
- SVD of band-projected density form factors enables geometry-based truncation of Hilbert space.
- CNOs are eigenstates of unit-cell reduced density matrix, measuring minimal orbital complexity.
- Applied to chiral TBG, dominant CNO at AA site resembles heavy-fermion f-electron.
- Subdominant CNOs can be treated at mean-field level; dominant requires dynamical self-energy.
- CNOs bridge band topology and real-space correlations, aiding analysis of emergent phases.
