## Featured Papers

### [Objective vs. Search: Decomposing What Makes a Good Tokeniser](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19145v1)
**Authors:** Ahmetcan Yavuz, Clara Meister, Tiago Pimentel
**Published:** 2026-09-16
**Summary:** Search procedure, not optimization objective, dominates tokenizer quality: bottom-up tokenizers yield lower bits-per-byte than top-down ones.

**What's new**
- Introduces BottomUpLL and TopDownComp to complete a 2x2 design space of objective vs. search.
- Disentangles compression vs. log-likelihood objectives from bottom-up vs. top-down search.
- Finds search procedure is the dominant factor: bottom-up tokenizers consistently lower bits-per-byte.
- BLiMP results show no consistent relationship between design choice and performance.
- Offers concrete guidance for more principled tokenizer construction.

### [A Zeroth-Order Paradigm for LLM Preference Alignment](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19144v1)
**Authors:** Peter Chen, Xi Chen, Wotao Yin, Tianyi Lin
**Published:** 2026-09-16
**Summary:** ComPO is a zeroth-order preference alignment method using comparison oracles that avoids differentiable preference losses and mitigates likelihood displacement.

**What's new**
- Introduces ComPO, a zeroth-order alignment method based on comparison oracles rather than differentiable preference losses.
- Provides convergence guarantees for offline ComPO under smoothness, gradient sparsity, and oracle-objective compatibility.
- Proposes online ComPO using unlabeled policy generations for reverse-KL control relative to a reference policy.
- Establishes performance guarantees under local coverage and in-distribution pairwise reward accuracy.
- Demonstrates improvements over direct alignment methods on Mistral, Llama, Gemma-2, Qwen3, and Gemma-3.
