### [User Embeddings are Superpositions of Interpretable Behavioral Modes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08487v1)
**Authors:** Jonathan Kozaczuk, Zheng Dong, Noyan Evirgen, Keivan Majidi, Eddie K. H. Ng, Chris Porter, Qi Zhao, Jia Geng, Changmao Li, Brandon R. Feng
**Published:** 2026-11-05
**Summary:** User embeddings can be decomposed into sparse superpositions of interpretable behavioral modes via sparse autoencoders, enabling natural-language labeling and zero-shot audience retrieval.

**What's new**
- Frames user embeddings as sparse superpositions of interpretable behavioral modes, grounded in generative and superposition theory.
- Uses sparse autoencoders to recover modes, outperforming classical sparse baselines on synthetic and transformer-encoded data.
- Validates on classical and transformer-based embeddings from two real-world datasets.
- Natural-language mode labels enable zero-shot audience retrieval and characterization without text-trained embeddings.
- Method is post-hoc and model-agnostic, requiring no changes to the upstream embedding system.

### [A physics-based-preconditioned quasi-Newton solver for energy- and momentum-conserving implicit hybrid particle-in-cell simulations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08402v1)
**Authors:** Prabhat Kumar, Luis Chacón, William J. White, Lujain Almarhabi, Stephen E. Clark
**Published:** 2026-11-05
**Summary:** A fast quasi-Newton solver enables energy- and momentum-conserving implicit hybrid PIC simulations, avoiding numerical heating and finite-grid instabilities.

**What's new**
- Nonlinear residual uses electric field and electron pressure as unknowns, nonlinearly eliminating particles.
- Approximate Jacobian replaces ion particle linear response with a local analytical mass matrix requiring minimal storage.
- Physics-based multigrid preconditioner adapted from Hall MHD efficiently inverts the approximate Jacobian.
- On GEM reconnection, explicit integration needs 40x smaller time step and 9x wall-clock time for comparable energy conservation.
- Applied to 3D field-reversed configuration formation by external coils.
