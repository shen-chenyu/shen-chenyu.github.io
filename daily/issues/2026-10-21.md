# Daily Issue (JST): 2026-10-21

## What's New Today

**Agentic AI dominates: memory, verification, and world models; materials MLIPs stress-tested**

This batch is overwhelmingly AI/ML, with a strong cluster on agent harnesses, memory, and verification, plus several papers questioning whether language pretraining or benchmark scores are necessary. Materials and physics work focuses on out-of-domain reliability of foundation MLIPs, thermomigration in tungsten, and cavity-controlled photophysics.

**Highlights**
- Halcyon shows strong text-to-image generation without language pretraining, using far less compute than baselines.
- Agent harnesses are a recurring theme: OpenVLHarness, WildAWM, HarnessPro, and studies of context truncation and retrieval costs.
- Memory work spans MemT-1.0, BetaMem, ScopeMem, and a geometric analysis of retrieval-bound memory failures.
- Benchmarks proliferate: FunCADBench for functional CAD, ProteinGeoBench, StepBench, WhatIf-Bench, and KEEP-Bench for compression faithfulness.
- Foundation MLIPs fail on disordered carbons despite low DFT errors, highlighting out-of-domain risks.
- Safety and alignment papers examine scheming, honeypots for insider threats, safety mid-training, and jailbreak evaluation pitfalls.

**Themes**

### [Text-to-Image Generative Modeling Without Language Pretraining](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26863v1)
**Authors:** Keshigeyan Chandrasegaran, Yue Zhao, Armin W. Thomas, Ehsan Adeli, Jiajun Wu, Juan Carlos Niebles, Li Fei-Fei, Michael Poli
**Published:** 2026-10-19
**Summary:** Halcyon shows strong text-to-image generation can be trained from scratch without language pretraining, beating baselines with far less compute.

**What's new**
- Introduces Halcyon, early-fusion multimodal autoregressive T2I models pretrained from scratch on 100M image-text pairs.
- Halcyon-0.52B beats LlamaGen-XL/8 (48.0 vs 63.3 gFD) with 18.2x less pretraining compute.
- Halcyon-1.6B beats JiT-T2I-XXL/8++ (35.3 vs 41.0 gFD) with 275x less pretraining compute.
- Controlled experiments show text-only pretraining does not improve T2I generation.
- Replacing caption dropout with I2T supervision yields image captioning while keeping competitive T2I quality.

### [Reinforcement Learning with Visually Attributable On-Policy Self-Distillation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26856v1)

### [Same Simulation, Different Evidence: Where Retrieved Tool Output Costs LLM Agents Accuracy](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26836v1)
**Authors:** Ke Zhang, Sahchit Chundur, Jerry Peprah Owusu, Mohammad Javad Abdolhosseini Qomi, Maziar Raissi
**Published:** 2026-10-19
**Summary:** Retrieving tool output instead of inlining it costs GPT models accuracy via evidence substitution, while Claude models show no detectable cost.

**What's new**
- Section-index retrieval of PHREEQC reports drops GPT-5.1 and GPT-5.2 accuracy by 11.3 and 7.9 points; Claude models unaffected.
- Re-running 11,007 logged simulator calls shows GPT models still compute the keyed value; they answer from wrong evidence instead.
- Evidence substitution: agents skip the report, use their own higher-precision output files, and misround to a neighboring option.
- Naming the authoritative output recovers 6.3-7.9 points for three GPT models; asking for correct rounding does not help.
- Harness context-management choices should be judged by where accuracy is lost, not only by final accuracy.

### [Topological Sheaves of Irreducibility: A Theoretical Framework for Multi-Agent Task Obstructions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26777v1)
**Authors:** Steven Motta, Davide Mambelli, Alessandro Rozza
**Published:** 2026-10-19
**Summary:** TSI is a sheaf-theoretic framework that separates task-level compatibility obstructions from system capability in multi-agent systems via cohomology.

**What's new**
- Introduces Topological Sheaves of Irreducibility (TSI) using interaction complex K and capacity sheaf F.
- Nonzero H^n(K;F) defines a structural signature; cochain realization is exact iff the cohomology class vanishes.

### [Vacancy and Helium-Cluster Thermomigration in Tungsten](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26667v1)
**Authors:** Sanad Alturk, Enrique Martinez
**Published:** 2026-10-19
**Summary:** Atomistic jump-statistics simulations quantify thermomigration of a vacancy and He1-He4 clusters in tungsten, finding all defects migrate toward hot regions with defect-specific heat-of-transport coefficients.

**What's new**
- Extends a hydrogen jump-statistics framework to vacancy and He1-He4 thermomigration in bcc tungsten.
- All defects show negative alpha, meaning net migration toward the hot region.
- He4 has the largest response (alpha = -2.2457e-2 K^-1), followed closely by the vacancy.
- He3 is the least mobile cluster with the largest effective migration energy (0.271 eV).
- Thermomigration cannot be inferred from cluster size, diffusivity, or migration energy alone.
