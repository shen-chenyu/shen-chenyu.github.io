## Featured Papers

### [Reliable AI-Assisted Decisions via Selective Auditing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31662v1)
**Authors:** Isaac Gibbs, Elise L. Han, Ryan J. Tibshirani, Tijana Zrnic
**Published:** 2026-10-22
**Summary:** Online method decides when to auto-accept AI decisions vs. consult experts, provably capping unaudited errors while minimizing auditing frequency.

**What's new**
- Frames selective auditing as online constrained optimization with a user-set threshold on unaudited AI errors.
- Derives algorithms with error-control guarantees under no assumptions, including an optimally efficient variant.
- Optimal method asymptotically attains the best achievable auditing rate for both adversarial and i.i.d. streams.
- Experiments on LLM harmful-request screening, text annotation, and data labeling show valid control with far less human auditing.

### [EpiSEM-Bench: Evaluating Safety and Evidence Recovery in LLM Agents under Missing Evidence](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31650v1)
**Authors:** Qijin Zhang, Hanzhen Wang, Yuehan Zhao, Haoyu Dai, Yao Shu, Zhongxiang Dai, Shuang Qiu
**Published:** 2026-10-22
**Summary:** EpiSEM-Bench is an executable benchmark of 300 matched unsafe/safe task pairs testing whether LLM agents avoid prohibited actions and recover missing safety facts.

**What's new**
- Introduces a benchmark for unsafe execution under critical evidence omission across six domains.
- Measures minimum observed failure cardinality, including full-evidence failures and no-failure cases.
- Uses an independent audit tool to test evidence seeking and withholding of prohibited actions.
- Finds large variation across ten models in omission robustness, evidence acquisition, and appropriate action.
- Shows resistance to missing evidence does not imply useful recovery, though recovery access cuts unsafe execution for all models.

### [Low-Bit N-Gram Embeddings for Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31646v1)
**Authors:** Stephen Chung, Wenyu Du
**Published:** 2026-10-22
**Summary:** Storing n-gram embedding tables at 4-bit precision cuts table memory ~75% while keeping over 99% of the validation-loss gains.

**What's new**
- Four-bit n-gram tables save ~75% of table memory while preserving >99% of validation-loss improvement.
- Fewer than four bits loses more performance, but a short fine-tuning phase recovers much of it.
- Reinvesting saved memory into larger low-bit tables can beat full-precision tables at lower memory.
- On DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash and Qwen3.8-Flash-Next, low-bit tables save 47-55% of table memory.
- Perplexity rises under 1.2% on those large models, with downstream task performance largely preserved.

### [A Fix for Softmax and Cross-Entropy](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31642v1)
**Authors:** Jonathan T. Barron, Dan Gnanapragasam, Michael Tschannen
**Published:** 2026-10-22
**Summary:** Paper identifies two numerical bugs in standard softmax/log-softmax/cross-entropy implementations and provides simple fixes that improve training stability and distillation.

**What's new**
- Fixes Softmax Collapse, where a dominant target logit gets zero gradient and gradients stop summing to zero
- Fixes Softmax Cancellation, which yields nonzero gradients when student and teacher logits are equal
- Eliminates the Slingshot phenomenon and speeds up grokking with weight decay
- Prevents accuracy collapse without weight decay and stops student drift during distillation
- Shows post-memorization logit growth is bounded by equilibrium between weight decay and Adam's epsilon

### [BeNice! Closing Looping's Inference-Memory Gap via KV-Sharing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31633v1)
**Authors:** Hayden Prairie, Jacob M. Ryan, Taylor Berg-Kirkpatrick, Daniel Y. Fu
**Published:** 2026-10-22
**Summary:** BeNice! enables KV-cache sharing across recurrences in looped models via stochastic-depth pretraining plus post-training, cutting inference memory with no quality loss.

**What's new**
- Identifies KV-shareability as inconsistent across identically trained looped models and studies how to enable it.
- Stage 1: stochastic depth during pretraining induces contractive residual dynamics toward a common representation limit.
- Stage 2: BeNice! broadcasts the final recurrence's KV-cache into the first, training compatible residuals.
- Matches standard finetuning's unshared validation perplexity while enabling KV-sharing.
- Composes with MLA, Kimi delta attention, and fp8/mxfp8/nvfp4 quantization; up to 31.4% better cache hits and 83.6% lower p90 TTFT.

### [Spin-Dependent Transport in One-Dimensional Mercury- and Silver-Mediated DNA Nanowires](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31508v1)
**Authors:** Arpan De, William Livernois, Simon Vecchioni, Yoel P. Ohayon, M. P. Anantram
**Published:** 2026-10-22
**Summary:** Heavy-metal-coordinated DNA nanowires form broad metal-centered bands, with Hg2+ enabling strong spin-flip conductance and Ag+ weak spin-flip scattering.

**What's new**
- Periodic heavy-metal coordination creates dispersive metal-centered bands, not isolated localized states, in DNA nanowires.
- Conduction bandwidth approaches 1 eV, over twice canonical DNA, aided by second nearest-neighbor hopping.
- Spin-orbit coupling enhances band dispersion and yields effective SO splitting comparable to next-nearest-neighbor hopping.
- Hg2+-coordinated nanowires show spin-flip conductance comparable to spin-conserving conductance across the LUMO manifold.
- Ag+-coordinated nanowires show spin-flip scattering over an order of magnitude weaker than spin-preserving transport.

### [Prompting Strategy Effects Depend on the Judge: A Multilingual Study of LLM-as-a-Judge Robustness](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31172v1)
**Authors:** Asal Mehrabi, Mohammad Aliannejadi, Clemencia Siro
**Published:** 2026-10-22
**Summary:** Prompting strategy effects in LLM-as-a-Judge are not transferable across judge models or languages; the judge model dominates, and effects can reverse direction.

**What's new**
- Benchmarked 9 prompting strategies, 4 open-weight judges, 27 languages, 4 tasks, 13 datasets, over 1M pointwise scores.
- Judge model choice dominates prompting strategy choice in multilingual evaluation.
- Strategy effects reverse direction across judges, so English-based recommendations do not transfer.
- Stability degrades in low-resource languages, with effects hidden by aggregate reporting.
- Prompting strategies should be validated per evaluation setting; cross-study comparisons must account for judge and prompt config.

### [Who Is Sycophantic to Whom? Confident LLMs, Human Deference, and the Collapse of Trust Calibration](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31579v1)
**Authors:** Deepthika S, Pawni Yadav, B. Sankar
**Published:** 2026-10-22
**Summary:** Users absorbed confident LLM falsehoods about 65% of the time, held them with high confidence, and lost the ability to tell right from wrong, while trust ratings barely changed.

**What's new**
- Reverses sycophancy: tests whether humans bend toward confident, consistent, wrong LLMs rather than the reverse.
- 32 participants, 104 quizzes, 1,040 answers; scores fell from 7.4 to 2.9 out of 10 as falsification rose.
- Planted falsehoods chosen on 60% of falsified items vs 11% of correct ones (odds ratio 12.5).
- Confidence discrimination for correctness dropped from AUROC 0.70 to chance; absorbed falsehoods held at 8.4/10 confidence.
- Introduces a baseline-corrected Human Deference Index; argues trustworthiness is a property of the human-AI pair.

### [Evaluating the Applicability of LLM Inference Optimizations on Apple Silicon](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31479v1)
**Authors:** Kyrylo Yemets, Andrii Yaroshevych, Victor Muryn, Ostap Khomenko, Pavlo Kryven, Maksym Kmet, Maksym Shamrai, Mariya Hirna
**Published:** 2026-10-22
**Summary:** Apple Silicon LLM inference optimizations transfer unevenly: KV-cache pruning helps, speculative decoding hurts, and GPU kernel and quantization gains depend on hardware and workload.

**What's new**
- KV-cache pruning reaches up to 1.75x end-to-end throughput on Apple but can reduce throughput on desktop NVIDIA GPUs.
- All evaluated speculative decoding methods reduce throughput on Apple, despite reported 2-6x gains on server GPUs.
- CUDA kernel designs transfer only partially to Metal; transferable components yield up to 1.20x decode throughput.
- Quantization gains vary by machine across four Apple systems.
- Speedups are not portable properties of a technique but joint functions of technique, runtime, hardware contract, and workload regime.

- Defines State Integrity Collapse: constraint retained in memory but no longer applied to later turns.
