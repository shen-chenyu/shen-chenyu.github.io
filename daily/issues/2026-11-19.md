### [RSIBench-Infra: Can Agents Optimize Their Own Training and Inference Infrastructure at Production Scale?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24395v1)
**Authors:** Hanlin Wu, Yifei Wang, Zhicheng Cai, Huan-ang Gao, Wei-Ying Ma, Ya-Qin Zhang, Jingjing Liu, Hao Zhou
**Published:** 2026-11-18
**Summary:** RSIBench-Infra benchmarks whether AI agents can optimize end-to-end training and inference infrastructure at production scale across 20 tasks on up to 32 GPUs/NPUs.

**What's new**
- Introduces a 20-task benchmark spanning diverse model families and modalities for agent-driven infra optimization.
- Deployments scale to 32 accelerators on NVIDIA GPUs and Ascend NPUs, unlike layer-specific benchmarks.
- Agents start from runnable official-recipe baselines and can modify engine, kernel, and instruction levels.
- An OCI image-layer submission protocol enables reproducible deployment and independent end-to-end evaluation.
- 66.2% of task results exceed 1.10x validated speedup, though consistent end-to-end gains remain hard.

### [PIPETTE: Prompt Injection Purple-Teaming for Robust LLM Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24362v1)
**Authors:** Lipeng He, Yihan Wang, Jiawen Zhang, N. Asokan
**Published:** 2026-11-18
**Summary:** PIPETTE is a purple-teaming framework that turns prompt-injection red-teaming discoveries into real defender robustness via dictionary-learning rewards and on-policy adversarial training.

**What's new**
- Shows high attack success rate in RL red-teaming does not guarantee robustness gains from adversarial training.
- Identifies mode collapse in RL-Hammer and PISmith, which repeatedly find similar attack strategies.
- Introduces a dictionary learning-based reward encouraging broader coverage of attack strategies.
- Integrates an on-policy adversarial training pipeline tailored to indirect prompt injection defense.
- Achieves near-zero attack success on static attacks and resists strong adaptive attacks while keeping benign utility.

### [AutoPI: Taxonomy-Guided Adaptive Red Teaming for Prompt Injection in LLM Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24313v1)
**Authors:** Stavros Damianakis, Konstantinos Psounis
**Published:** 2026-11-18
**Summary:** AutoPI is a black-box, training-free adaptive red teamer that uses the PIAtlas taxonomy to find diverse prompt-injection attacks and measure technique coverage, not just attack success rate.

**What's new**
- Reframes red teaming around technique coverage rather than only attack success rate (ASR).
- Uses the PIAtlas taxonomy to guide search and measure class, technique, and sub-technique coverage.
- Attacker LLM writes injections per sub-technique and iteratively reworks failures using LLM-judge feedback.
- Plugs into existing benchmarks in place of fixed attack templates and stays current as taxonomy evolves.
- On AgentDojo and IPI Arena, beats recent adaptive red teamers on ASR and class-level coverage without training.

### [MoE-Context: Show the Model Only What It Needs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24379v1)
**Authors:** Vedant Navale
**Published:** 2026-11-18
**Summary:** MoE-Context splits context into blocks and uses a fast yes/no pass to show the model only needed blocks, masking the rest in a single KV cache.

**What's new**
- Context split into blocks; a fast yes/no pass selects needed blocks before each step, rest kept as one-line notes.
- Hidden blocks are masked rather than deleted, over a single key-value cache.
- On Qwen3-1.7B synthetic lookup, showing only needed block raised accuracy from 93 to 119 of 120.
- Model's own block picking is weak; a supervised linear probe lifts it from 80 to 110 of 120 but doesn't transfer.
- Enables cheap patterns: undoing a failed attempt and running subagents without a new context.

### [Semantic Context Handoff with On-Demand Evidence for Long-Horizon Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24357v1)

**Published:** 2026-11-18
**Summary:** SCH separates a compact semantic state from addressable action-observation evidence, letting long-horizon agents recover relevant past evidence on demand to cut context cost and boost accuracy.

**What's new**
- Introduces state-evidence separation: compact semantic state plus separately addressable evidence that re-enters context when relevant.
- Avoids separate summarization or retrieval-model calls for evidence recovery.

- Trajectory analysis shows recovery of evidence from much earlier history, not just a recent context window.

### [LoopCache: Accelerating Long-Context Self-Speculative Decoding for Looped Transformers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24301v1)

**What's new**

### [Mesh Sensitivity of Different Boundary Treatments in High-Order Compact Gas-Kinetic Scheme for Hypersonic Heat-Flux Prediction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24392v1)
**Authors:** Xingyi Jiang, Jiawang Zhang, Junlei Mu, Xing Ji
**Published:** 2026-11-18
**Summary:** Compares three GKS wall treatments for Mach 8.03 cylinder heat flux across seven meshes; no treatment is uniformly best, GG most robust except under log-layer unpacked grids.

**What's new**
- Benchmarks linear Green-Gauss (GG), compact ghost-cell (GC), and pointwise one-sided compact (POS) GKS wall treatments.
- GG has lowest RMS heat-flux error on reference, uniform-reduction, and log-layer packed meshes.

### [Structure and Thermal Conductivity of Epoxy-Graphene Oxide Composites from 4.5 to 300 K](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24391v1)
**Authors:** A. Jeżowski, A. I. Krivchikov, O. A. Korolyuk, V. V. Sagan, Y. V. Horbatenko, S. V. Cherednychenko, D. Szewczyk
**Published:** 2026-11-18
**Summary:** Epoxy-graphene oxide composites show GO content reshapes thermal transport temperature dependence from 4.5-300 K rather than simply raising conductivity.

**What's new**
- XRD shows amorphous scattering with no GO(001) reflection, indicating suppressed ordered GO stacking in cured epoxy.
- Raman confirms growing carbon-phase contribution without pronounced changes to epoxy vibrational bands.
- Thermal conductivity (4.5-300 K) is fit by a multichannel model with a plateau term plus 2-3 Arrhenius contributions.
- The low-temperature conductivity plateau seen at low GO loading disappears in GO-6 and GO-10 samples.
- Results link structural organization to redistribution of effective thermal-transport channels in the composites.

### [Reconciling Models for Infinite-Layer Nickelate Superconductors: Role of Interstitial-$s$ Band or $A$-Pocket](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24288v1)
**Authors:** Ao Zhang, Chao Deng, Leonard M. Verhoff, Mi Jiang, Run Yang, Zhixiang Shi, Karsten Held, Liang Si
**Published:** 2026-11-18
**Summary:** DFT+DMFT shows interstitial-s band is negligible for Ni d_x2-y2 correlations, so single-band Hubbard models suffice for infinite-layer nickelate superconductivity.

**What's new**
- Compares 1-band, 5-band, and 6-band effective models for LaNiO2 using DFT+DMFT.
- Ni-3d 5-band models overestimate Hundness; 6-band models with interstitial-s recover Mottness.
- Interstitial-s band has negligible influence on d_x2-y2 correlations.
- Single-band Hubbard framework reliably describes superconductivity in infinite-layer nickelates.
- Accurate inter-orbital properties like optical conductivity require at least the interstitial-s band.
