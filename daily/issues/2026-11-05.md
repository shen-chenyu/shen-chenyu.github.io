# Daily Issue (JST): 2026-11-05

## What's New Today

**Graphs, memory, and routing dominate agentic LLM research; MLIPs and phonons advance materials simulation**

Today's papers show a strong push toward making LLM agents more capable and controllable through graph-structured reasoning, memory systems, and adaptive routing. In parallel, materials and physics work leverages fine-tuned foundation models and first-principles methods to probe ion dynamics, thermal transport, and phase transitions. Several papers also offer critical audits of evaluation practices, from confidence calibration to authorship impersonation.

**Highlights**
- Graph-mediated LLM systems survey introduces an operation-level taxonomy for reasoning, retrieval, and multi-agent coordination.
- Fine-tuned MACE model reveals temperature-dependent ion dynamics in argyrodite Li6PS5Cl, with localized phonon-like behavior below 500 K.
- Reward budgeting and conformal calibration address premature convergence and rollout inefficiency in RL for LLM reasoning.
- Prospective memory framework separates maintaining future intentions from deciding when to act in proactive LLM agents.
- First-principles study disentangles polar and Peierls modes in MOI2 ferroelectrics, showing TaOI2 is nonpolar despite Peierls distortions.
- Audits expose gaps in saliency map fairness for dense breasts and in risk-controlled fetal biometry, where rate certificates miss magnitude errors.

**What's new**

### [Reward Budgeting Reduces Premature Convergence in Reinforcement Learning for LLM Reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05942v1)
**Authors:** Mengni Jia, Mengyu Zhou, Xiaoxi Jiang, Guanjun Jiang
**Published:** 2026-11-04
**Summary:** Reward budgeting, a plug-in advantage construction with a fixed per-prompt reward budget, reduces premature convergence in RL for LLM reasoning without explicitly preserving entropy.

**What's new**
- Introduces a sharpness factor kappa and effective kappa_eff linking reward/advantage gaps to probability-ratio gaps.
- Proposes reward budgeting: fixed total reward budget per prompt, with advantage gap shrinking as more rollouts succeed.
- Shows unclipped estimator follows gradient of an exact finite-G concave objective with diminishing returns for binary rewards.
- Improves RL baselines on Qwen3-8B and Llama3.2-3B-Instruct, up to +12.50 on AMC23 and +10.00 on AIME24/25.
- Argues premature convergence stems from update dynamics, not entropy alone.

**Summary:** MAEP is a training-free modality-aware expert pruning framework that cuts MoE-MLLM expert memory ~47% while preserving multimodal performance.

**What's new**

### [Do Saliency Maps Fail Dense Breasts? A Chance-Normalized, Confounder-Aware Audit of Explanation Fairness in Mammography](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05909v1)
**Authors:** Ahmed Jaheen
**Published:** 2026-11-04
**Summary:** Audit finds saliency maps locate cancers worse in dense breasts, but case mix explains much of the gap; occlusion retains a significant residual deficit.

**What's new**
- Grad-CAM peaks hit 44% of dense vs 58% of non-dense lesions across three CNNs on 1,345 CBIS-DDSM views.

**Authors:** Piyush Tiwary, Anushka Srivastava, Prathosh A. P
**Published:** 2026-11-04
**Summary:** Introduces auditability as a fourth unlearning axis and proposes GapWiden plus GapVeil to give providers evidence of deletion across eleven methods.

**What's new**

### [Risk Control Certifies Rates, Not Magnitudes: An Audit of Selective Fetal Biometry](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05851v1)
**Authors:** Zhengshuai Yang, Linna Jiang, Changyun Li, Guihu Zhao
**Published:** 2026-11-04
**Summary:** Selective-prediction risk control certifies error rates but not magnitudes; anatomical admissibility bounds cut worst-case fetal HC errors from 271 mm to ~11 mm.

**What's new**
- Audit of tool-grounded HC18 biometry pipeline across two pre-registered four-way splits with three retrainings each.
- Clopper-Pearson risk control matched a hand-set floor yet still emitted a 271.3 mm error on a geometrically complete ring.
- Completeness score of 0.94 missed the failure; one outlier among ~150 answers left the α=0.10 bound intact.
- Raising the anatomical lower bound from 50 to 55 mm cost 0.4 coverage points and cut worst-case error to 11.4/11.1 mm.
- Suppressing via the completeness threshold instead cost 19.0/12.1 coverage points; at 70 mm MAE was 1.91/1.81 mm.

### [Ferroelectricity and Peierls transition in MOI$_2$ (M=Ta, Nb): a first-principle analysis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05836v1)
**Authors:** Carmel Dansou, Charles Paillard, Laurent Bellaiche
**Published:** 2026-11-04
**Summary:** First-principles study shows the polar instability in NbOI2 is a distinct order parameter from Peierls dimerization, with three coupled modes governing the MOI2 family.

**What's new**
- Polar distortion in NbOI2 is not caused by Peierls mode condensation but is a separate order parameter.
- Three key modes identified: polar P, antipolar eta, and Peierls delta, with metal-dependent couplings.
- TaOI2 is isostructural and shows Peierls distortions but is not polar, unlike NbOI2.
- Landau-type energy analysis reveals stability and coupling differences across the MOI2 family.

**What's new**
