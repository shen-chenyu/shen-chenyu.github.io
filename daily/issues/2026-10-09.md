# Daily Issue (JST): 2026-10-09

## What's New Today

**Agent Memory, Distillation Limits, and Materials Discovery Dominate Today's Papers**

Today's submissions cluster around making LLM agents more reliable through better memory, distillation, and verification, while materials and physics papers push machine-learned potentials and inverse design. A recurring theme is diagnosing when a method's gains are real versus artifacts of evaluation or training dynamics.

**Highlights**
- DeltaReplay proposes step-level memory reuse for mobile GUI agents, deciding relevance by task relation rather than record content.
- Multiple papers dissect on-policy distillation: DIAL-OPD selects tokens by learning value, while others show offline student rollouts can beat on-policy sampling.
- TRACE and related work turn verifier score changes into testable diagnoses, exposing brittleness in agentic evaluation.
- Materials papers use ML force fields and CNNs to predict plasmonic poles, CDW mechanisms, and validate crystallographic databases.
- Diffusion model studies probe memorization onset via basin geometry and perturbation propagation theory.
- Agent safety and reliability get attention through organizational behavior framing, trust-boundary delimiters, and failure attribution.

## Featured Papers

### [DeltaReplay: Task-Relative Memory Reuse for Mobile GUI Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11707v1)
**Authors:** Yudong Bai, Yihong Chen, Quanming Yao, Yaqing Wang
**Published:** 2026-10-08
**Summary:** DeltaReplay is a step-level memory reuse framework for mobile GUI agents that decides per step whether to follow, adapt, or ignore stored trajectories, improving success rates.

**What's new**
- Stores execution trajectories as paths in a transition graph with pages as nodes and actions as edges.
- Splits each action into task-independent operation and task-specific parameters for flexible reuse.
- At reuse time, compares each recorded step with the new task and current screen to decide follow, adapt, or ignore.
- Improves task success rate by up to 10.3 points on AndroidWorld and 25.0 points on SPA-Bench.

### [Predicting plasmonic pole representations of complex dielectric spectra using convolutional neural networks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11698v1)
**Authors:** Dario A. Leon
**Published:** 2026-10-08
**Summary:** A CNN trained on synthetic multipole-Padé spectra directly inverts dielectric spectra into plasmonic pole parameters in one forward pass, generalizing to real data.

**What's new**
- Direct CNN inversion of dielectric spectra into plasmonic pole structures via multipole-Padé representation.
- Trained only on synthetic spectra with randomized parameters, avoiding material-specific datasets.
- Generalizes to first-principles and experimental dielectric spectra with high accuracy.
- Single forward pass replaces computationally demanding, initialization-sensitive nonlinear fitting.
- Enables high-throughput automated analysis of dielectric spectra across diverse materials.

### [Mode-selective electron-phonon coupling drives charge density waves in the kagome metals YRu$_3$Si$_2$ and LaRu$_3$Si$_2$](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11689v1)
**Authors:** Wenqian Tu, Run Lv, Dingfu Shao, Yuping Sun, Wenjian Lu
**Published:** 2026-10-08
**Summary:** Mode-selective electron-phonon coupling, not Fermi-surface nesting, drives record-high CDW transitions in kagome metals YRu3Si2 and LaRu3Si2.

**What's new**
- Identifies mode-selective EPC as the CDW mechanism in YRu3Si2 and LaRu3Si2
- Rules out Fermi-surface nesting; electronic susceptibility is featureless
- Ru out-of-plane phonons couple Ru-dxz/dyz and Si-px/py orbitals
- Phonon anharmonicity melts the CDW and reproduces Q_CDW and T_CDW
- Smaller Y3+ radius strengthens Ru-Ru bonds, doubling T_CDW vs LaRu3Si2

### [TRACE: Diagnosing Verifier Brittleness in Agentic Evaluation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11678v1)
**Authors:** Radhika Gaonkar
**Published:** 2026-10-08
**Summary:** TRACE is a protocol that diagnoses whether agent score changes reflect real capability shifts or verifier brittleness from evaluation changes.

**What's new**
- Renaming tools drops a scripted agent's score by 0.250 despite identical operations; rescoring restores the gap.
- On 88 tau2-bench tasks, renaming tools or reformatting outputs left reward unchanged within ±0.10 for 7 of 8 pairs.
- Deliberately misleading tool names lower every agent's reward by 0.20-0.44, confirming real effects are detectable.
- Identical reruns flip 15-36% of task outcomes, so single-run comparisons cannot separate presentation effects from noise.
- Two frontier LLM judges agree on reformatted trajectories but disagree with each other on 57% of records.

### [Early Signatures of Memorization in Diffusion Models via Basin Geometry and Cyclic Denoising](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11670v1)
**Authors:** Nikhil Verma, Siddharthan Dileep, Anoop Singh, Srikanth Sastry, Ramya Hebbalaguppe, Sayan Ranu, N. M. Anoop Krishnan
**Published:** 2026-10-08
**Summary:** Memorization in diffusion models is encoded in energy landscape geometry before it appears in generated samples, detectable via basin volume and cyclic denoising.

**What's new**
- Latent memorization: localized basins form around training samples before any memorized outputs appear.
- Cyclic denoising recovers training images from checkpoints with no one-shot copies.
- 500 cycles raise memorized fraction above 30% at a checkpoint with 0.1% one-shot copies.
- Cycling reveals degenerate attractors that fade, so basin residence does not imply memorization.
- Method extends to Stable Diffusion v1.4, separating memorized prompts with AUC 0.944.

### [DIAL-OPD: Learning More from Fewer Tokens in On-Policy Distillation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11659v1)
**Authors:** Anhao Zhao, Haoran Xin, Junlong Tong, Yingqi Fan, Xuan Lu, Ping Nie, Wenjie Li, Xiaoyu Shen
**Published:** 2026-10-08
**Summary:** DIAL-OPD selects tokens by learning value using log-probability weighting, outperforming full-token on-policy distillation with only 40% of tokens.

**What's new**
- Fewer tokens can outperform full-token OPD, challenging the intuition that more supervision improves learning.
- DIAL-OPD weights reward magnitude by the logarithmic mean of teacher and student probabilities.
- Retaining 40% of tokens yields mean accuracy gains up to 5.25 points over Vanilla OPD.
- Doubles AIME25 Pass@16 from 13.33% to 26.67% and beats strongest token-selection baseline by 18%.
- With a 4B teacher, DIAL-OPD surpasses the strongest full-token baseline using an 8B teacher.

**What's new**

**Published:** 2026-10-08
**Summary:** LS-LoRA selects layers with low input-output cosine similarity for LoRA adaptation, improving target-task performance while retaining more general capabilities than all-layer LoRA.

**What's new**
- Fine-tuning different Transformer layers yields varying target-task gains and capability degradation.

- LS-LoRA places LoRA adapters only in low-similarity layers, improving target performance and retaining commonsense reasoning.
