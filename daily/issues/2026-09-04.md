## Featured Papers

### [A Common Measure of Communication for Speech Brain-Computer Interfaces](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02887v1)
**Authors:** Dulhan Jayalath, Benjamin Ballyk, Oiwi Parker Jones
**Published:** 2026-09-02
**Summary:** Introduces OVMI, an information-theoretic metric for fair comparison of speech BCIs across different vocabularies and conditions.

**What's new**
- Derives open-vocabulary mutual information (OVMI) to measure decoder information relative to a user's intended word distribution.
- Shows standard metrics like WER can overstate system performance when computed only over supported words.
- Uses OVMI to compare systems, revealing trade-offs between vocabulary coverage and decoding accuracy.
- Demonstrates vocabulary selection via OVMI improves accuracy by up to 16.3% across three speech domains.

### [Repairing PBE-Spurious Metallicity for HSE06-Level Screening of 2D Photocatalysts for Green Hydrogen Production](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02883v1)
**Authors:** Ritam Chakraborty, Arpan Das
**Published:** 2026-09-02
**Summary:** A machine-learning workflow repairs PBE-spurious metallicity in 2D materials to enable HSE06-level screening of photocatalysts for green hydrogen production.

**What's new**
- Two-stage pipeline: classifier for PBE metals, then Δ-learning to predict HSE06 band gaps.
- Curated XGBoost regressor achieves MAE 0.108 eV (R²=0.989) vs 1.036 eV for raw PBE.
- Corrected screening yields 10 strict and 29 relaxed photocatalyst candidates.
- Four strict and 18 relaxed candidates would be missed by PBE gap window.
- High-fidelity correction must be judged by candidate membership, not just global error.

### [Graph Machine: Towards Better Pretraining via Edges](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02881v1)
**Authors:** Lintai Hou
**Published:** 2026-09-02
**Summary:** Graph Machine (GM) is a new architecture using sparse, dynamic routing via edges to maintain O(n) state, achieving efficient pretraining with minimal loss.

**What's new**
- Introduces edges as pointer-like objects updated by a referral mechanism for dynamic routing.
- Maintains O(n) complexity in sparse layers while allowing O(n) accessible state size.
- Replaces 75% of dense Transformer layers in Qwen3-0.6B with GM sparse layers.
- Pretrains on 15.7B tokens, showing slight loss degradation with 2 tokens retrieved per KV head.
- With 4 tokens retrieved, the model marginally improves loss over dense baseline.

### [GRADSOLVE: fast exact gradients for ODE ensembles on GPUs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02876v1)
**Authors:** Alessio Spurio Mancini
**Published:** 2026-09-02
**Summary:** GRADSOLVE is a JAX library for fast exact reverse-mode gradients of ODE ensembles on GPUs, using fixed-step replay of adaptive solver steps.

**What's new**
- Records adaptive solver steps and differentiates a fixed-step replay for exact discrete adjoints.
- Achieves 5.6-14.1x faster gradients than Diffrax's checkpointed adjoint at matched accuracy.
- Forward-only kernel runs 2.8x faster than DiffEqGPU.jl.
- Supports explicit and Rosenbrock integrators with Diffrax fallback.

### [Low-Temperature Transport in Li-Ion Battery EC/EMC/FEC Electrolytes: Molecular Dynamics and Machine-Learning Modeling](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02824v1)
**Authors:** İpek Yenda Çınar, Oguzhan Orhan, M. Oluş Özbek, Şener Özönder
**Published:** 2026-09-02
**Summary:** MD and ML reveal that low-temperature Li-ion transport in EC/EMC/FEC electrolytes is dominated by solvent composition, with EMC causing severe conductivity loss and FEC acting as a secondary modifier.

**What's new**
- EMC-containing electrolytes show >98% conductivity drop at 233 K vs ~90% for EC-rich systems.
- Li+ diffusion activation energies: 0.49-0.54 eV (EMC) vs 0.27-0.29 eV (EC).

- Gaussian-process surrogates predict conductivity with RMSE ~0.56 mS/cm.
