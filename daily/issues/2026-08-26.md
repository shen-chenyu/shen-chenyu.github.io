## Featured Papers

### [How to Train a Critic Stably and Efficiently](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23566v1)
**Authors:** Penghui Qi, Xiangxin Zhou, Wee Sun Lee
**Published:** 2026-08-24
**Summary:** Introduces BPCO, a stable critic-based RL recipe for LLMs that matches group-based methods with one response per prompt.

**What's new**
- Combines DPPO, bounded value predictions, MC targets, unnormalized advantages, and length-adaptive GAE.
- Conditions critic on reward-defining info (e.g., reference answer) hidden from policy.
- Improves critic-based baselines across math tasks, matching/exceeding GRPO with single sampling.
- Validated on models from 1.5B to 30B-A3B MoE, including rubric-based rewards.

### [ReWorld: An Interactive World Model with Long-Horizon Memory](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23565v1)
**Authors:** Zhifei Chen, Luozhou Wang, Guibao Shen, Dongyu Yan, Shuai Yang, Tianshuo Xu, Yihua Du, Wei Wang, Tianyi Gui, Lianghua Huang, Yingcong Chen
**Published:** 2026-08-24
**Summary:** ReWorld is an interactive world model that separates control and memory via mixed attention heads and a bounded KV cache, enabling real-time, long-horizon video generation.

**What's new**
- Mixed per-head attention windows with global heads for long-horizon memory.
- Random head routing and chunk dropping to handle sparse histories.
- Bounded KV cache with pose-indexed landmark bank for fixed memory budget.
- Metric-scale-aligned data engine unifying eight sources on one action scale.
- LoRA-based distillation for 4-step sampling, enabling real-time 704x1280 video.

### [SWE Refactor Bench: Can Coding Agents Complete a Long-Horizon, Whole-Repository Stack Migration?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23564v1)
**Authors:** Deyao Hong, Yizhe Chi, Wenyi Li, Xiaoqiu Wang, Mingju Gao, Kaisen Yang, Bingxiang He, Youjie Zheng, Calvin Xiao, Qinhuai Na
**Published:** 2026-08-24
**Summary:** Introduces SWE Refactor Bench, a benchmark for evaluating coding agents on whole-repository migrations, revealing that current models rarely complete them correctly.

**What's new**
- Benchmark of 20 real-world repository migrations across 4 technical debt types.
- Three-stage evaluation: Migration Audit, Behavioural Tests, and Agentic Verification.
- Only 5.4% of 520 runs pass all stages; best model scores 47/100.
- Identifies 'Blindness' hack where agents copy original code to pass tests.
- Shows migration completeness and behavioural correctness are distinct abilities.

### [Mapping metallic d-wave altermagnetism across the TiNiSi structural family](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23409v1)
**Authors:** Zhen Zhang, Subhadip Pradhan, Kirill D. Belashchenko, Vladimir Antropov
**Published:** 2026-08-24
**Summary:** Identifies 16 metallic d-wave altermagnets in the TiNiSi family, including 12 new predictions, guided by an empirical magnetic motif.

**What's new**
- Empirical magnetic motif: ferromagnetic zigzag chains with antiferromagnetic interchain coupling.
- First-principles screening of 280 ternary systems yields 16 d-wave altermagnets.
- Recovers four known members and predicts 12 new ones, with six most promising.
- Noncollinear calculations confirm collinear altermagnetic ground states.
- Leading candidates show nonzero spin-splitter angle and anomalous Hall conductivity.

### [Highly Stable Glasses of cis-Decalin and cis/trans-Decalin Mixtures](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23443v1)
**Authors:** Katherine R. Whitaker, Daniel J. Scifo, M. D. Ediger, Mathias Ahrenberg, Christoph Schick
**Published:** 2026-08-24
**Summary:** Vapor-deposited decalin glasses show high stability, including first ultrastable molecular mixture.

**What's new**
- First report of highly stable glass formation from molecular mixtures.
- 50/50 cis/trans-decalin is highest fragility material to form ultrastable glass.
- Stable glasses have heat capacities up to 4.5% lower than liquid-cooled glass.
- Kinetic stability: transformation time ~10^4.4 times structural relaxation time.

### [First-Principles Electron-Magnon Coupling with Machine-Learning Hamiltonians: From Band Renormalization to Transport](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23333v1)

**Published:** 2026-08-24

**What's new**

### [Defect-Mediated Nucleation and Dynamics across the Phase Transition in the Excitonic Insulator Candidate Ta2NiSe5](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23438v1)
**Authors:** Guilherme Rodrigues-Fontenele, Gabriel Fontenele, Ângelo Malachias, Rogério Magalhães-Paniago
**Published:** 2026-08-24
**Summary:** STM/STS reveals that the phase transition in Ta2NiSe5 proceeds via nucleation and growth of domains, with defects acting as nucleation centers that alter the intrinsic anisotropy.

**What's new**
- Direct real-space imaging of coexisting monoclinic and orthorhombic domains across the transition.
- Orthorhombic phase nucleates anisotropically perpendicular to Ta-Ni-Ta chains in pristine regions.
- Point defects and step edges promote nucleation and suppress the intrinsic anisotropy.
- Confirms martensitic-like, spatially inhomogeneous transformation rather than continuous evolution.
