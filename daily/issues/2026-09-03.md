### [Beyond Scores: Understanding LLM-as-a-Judge Mechanisms in Summarization Evaluation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01604v1)
**Authors:** Himil Vasava, Ming Jiang
**Published:** 2026-09-01
**Summary:** This paper mechanistically analyzes LLM-as-a-judge in summarization, revealing a two-stage pipeline and how fine-tuning sculpts it.

**What's new**
- Introduces an eight-attack perturbation taxonomy and controlled generation pipeline to probe evaluator mechanisms.
- Identifies a two-stage evaluation process: early attention for error comparison, later MLP for rating integration.
- Shows decision crystallization at a specific late layer (L=26 for Themis, L=25 for Prometheus).
- Base-model control isolates fine-tuning effects: suppression of early MLP and advanced crystallization depth.

### [The Structure of Quantization Damage in LLMs: Why the Next Bit Should Be Spent Globally](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01587v1)
**Authors:** Jundong Hu, Shekar Ramachandran
**Published:** 2026-09-01
**Summary:** Quantization damage in LLMs is diffuse, not localized; spending precision budget globally on finer granularity beats local layer repair.

**What's new**
- Causal mixed-precision interventions across 9 models show damage recovery is diffuse, not concentrated in task circuits or weight stats.
- For 8/9 models, recovering 75% of accuracy gap needs ~half the layers; Qwen3-8B is the exception with sharp concentration.
- At matched precision budget, global finer granularity (group-128) outperforms local layer repair by 21-52 points across all compatible models.
- Residual quantization error is budget-limited; 8-bit is near-lossless across RTN, GPTQ, and AWQ.
- Peak recovery location correlates with architecture within a family, but not across families.

**Summary:** Embedding retrieval fails when surface form and meaning diverge, anchoring on literal tokens rather than structure, with LLM reranking recovering only partially.

**What's new**
- Strict Hit@1 is 0.0% for math retrieval under heavy disguise, despite correct item in top 10.
