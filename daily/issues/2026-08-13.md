### [A Quantum Roadmap for Softmax Attention: Exact Born-Rule Analogs for Softmax Attention on the Probability Simplex](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11173v1)

**Published:** 2026-08-11
**Summary:** This paper presents an exact quantum realization of softmax attention for probability simplex inputs, mapping all components to quantum operations.

**What's new**
- Exact component-wise quantum analog of softmax attention via Born-rule measurement
