## Featured Papers

### [Learning When to Trust via Selective Context Preference Optimization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06377v1)
**Authors:** Xian Sun, Wei Chow, Yingshuo Wang, Junhao Liu, Wei Gao, Qing Wu, Lingdong Kong
**Published:** 2026-08-06
**Summary:** Introduces MIST benchmark and SCOPE method to improve selective trust in language models, balancing robustness against misleading context with accuracy on trustworthy context.

**What's new**
- MIST: human-annotated benchmark with four matched conditions (clean, misleading, correct, irrelevant) per reasoning item.
- SC2W metric: counts how often misleading context flips a clean-correct answer to wrong.
- SCOPE: DPO-based training on balanced preference pairs across all conditions, not just misleading ones.
- Reduces SC2W on open models while preserving accuracy on clean, correct, or irrelevant context.

### [The Bitter Lesson of Tool Calling](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06370v1)
**Authors:** Ishan Patel, Sahil Sen, Elias Lumer, Vamse Kumar Subbiah
**Published:** 2026-08-06
**Summary:** Programmatic tool calling (code) matches or beats JSON tool calling across 14 LLMs on BFCL v4, with GPT-5.6 improving 10.6%.

**What's new**
- First systematic comparison of code-based vs JSON tool calling on BFCL v4.
- PTC outperforms JSON in 11/14 models, with GPT-5.6 gaining 10.6%.
- PTC excels in parallel fan-out (13/14) and resists context rot better.
- Results show PTC is a robust, viable alternative to JSON tool calling.

### [AV-AIVAT: 74x Cheaper Agent Evaluation with Certified Anytime-Valid Stopping in Imperfect-Information Games](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06362v1)
**Authors:** Boning Li, Yu Chen, Longbo Huang
**Published:** 2026-08-06
**Summary:** AV-AIVAT combines AIVAT variance reduction with anytime-valid confidence sequences for certified early stopping in agent evaluation, cutting required hands by up to 74x.

**What's new**
- Introduces anytime-valid AIVAT (AV-AIVAT) for certified early stopping in imperfect-information games.
- Uses online value model to avoid self-scoring corrections, ensuring validity.
- Asymptotic CS achieves median 74x fewer hands than raw outcomes at 95% confidence.
- Exact finite-sample certification via Empirical-Bernstein CS with structural payoff bounds.
- Demonstrates 1.37x median stopping-time ratio in HUNL with descriptive EB-CS runs.

### [The Low Frequency Trap: Video Language Models Fail at Simple Event Bookkeeping](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06361v1)
**Authors:** Sarvesh Baskar, Zikui Cai, Shayan Shabihi, Anirudh Satheesh, Muhammad R. Islam, Udari Madhushani Sehwag, Tom Goldstein, Furong Huang
**Published:** 2026-08-06
**Summary:** Video language models fail at simple event counting, especially for transient events, with accuracy dropping sharply as event count and frequency increase.

**What's new**
- Introduces trace-grounded parametric profiling for event counting in controlled video tasks.
- Reveals a staged temporal failure: persistent events are counted reliably up to 12 at low frequencies, but transient events show no reliable counting.
- Shows that increasing sampling rate inflates final scores without improving faithful event recovery.
- Demonstrates that prompting strategies yield limited gains, and real-world evaluations confirm the same low-count success bias.

### [Scalable estimation of VARMA models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06340v1)
**Authors:** Daniel Paulin, Victor Elvira
**Published:** 2026-08-06
**Summary:** Introduces a scalable VARMA estimation framework with per-iteration cost independent of series length, enabling practical use in high dimensions.

**What's new**
- Optimization iterations independent of series length T via sufficient statistics and Parseval identity.
- Partial-autocorrelation reparametrization ensures stationarity and invertibility by construction.
- Two point estimators: regularized least-squares and covariance-marginalized MAP, both with near-parametric rates.
- Extends to seasonal, exogenous (VARMAX), and rolling-window settings at same leading cost.
- Outperforms baselines in high dimensions (d=10-40) where classical MLE fails.

### [Nuclear-Spin Statistical Weights from Young Diagrams](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06339v1)
**Authors:** Eric Kubischta, Ian Teixeira
**Published:** 2026-08-06
**Summary:** A combinatorial method using Young diagrams and major-index branching rules computes nuclear-spin statistical weights for molecules with cyclic/dihedral permutation symmetry.

**What's new**
- Extends Schur-Weyl framework to cyclic and dihedral permutation subgroups.
- Uses Kraśkiewicz-Weyman-Adin-Roichman branching rule for S_N down to C_m.
- Derives spin species directly from Young tableaux via major index.
- Applies uniformly to XeOF4, SF4, benzene, deuterated benzene, and tropylium.
- Provides automatable framework for nuclear-spin symmetry analysis.

### [Optimal Rates for Learning with Monotone Adversaries](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06337v1)
**Authors:** Anay Mehrotra
**Published:** 2026-08-06
**Summary:** Learning with monotone adversaries incurs an extra log factor for VC dimension ≥2, making the minimax rate Θ((d/n)log(n/d)).

**What's new**
- Shows the logarithmic overhead in ERM is inherent for VC dimension ≥2, not an artifact.
- Establishes minimax rates: Θ(1/n) for d=1, Θ((d/n)log(n/d)) for d≥2.
- Extends results to Littlestone dimension, ruling out online-to-batch rates.
- Provides a simple improper learner achieving the optimal rate for d=1.
- All lower bounds stem from a single explicit construction.

### [Does FLAIR super-resolution erase or hallucinate small white-matter lesions?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06311v1)

**Published:** 2026-08-06
**Summary:** Super-resolution of FLAIR MRI can erase small white-matter lesions, though it still improves detection over thick slices; ECLARE performs best.

**What's new**
- First study to evaluate SR effects on WMH segmentation, focusing on lesion-level erasure and hallucination.
- Finds erasure of small lesions is the dominant effect, increasing with slice thickness, not hallucination.
- ECLARE, a self-supervised method, best preserves small lesion signal at 3mm and 5mm slice thicknesses.
- Multi-contrast INR offers no advantage over cubic interpolation for lesion preservation.
