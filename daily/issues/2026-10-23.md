## Featured Papers

### [LongZero: Enhancing Long-Context Reasoning in LLMs from Zero Data](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30132v1)
**Authors:** Zhennan Wan, Jianfei Chen
**Published:** 2026-10-21
**Summary:** LongZero lets an LLM improve long-context reasoning via RL from zero data by self-synthesizing compact QA with evidence, then expanding it into long multi-document contexts.

**What's new**
- First RL-from-zero-data method targeting long-context reasoning, requiring no external corpora or teacher models.
- Synthesizes question, answer, and supporting evidence compactly first, then expands evidence into long multi-document context.
- Combines self-written multi-hop reasoning tasks with self-written multi-round retrieval tasks, trained via GRPO.
- Outperforms RL on externally synthesized corpus data across five long-context benchmarks on two LLMs.

### [Think Harder Where It Matters: Item-Level Adaptive Recursion for Sequential Recommendation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30128v1)
**Authors:** Zhanshuo Cao, Jiakai Tang, Yuqi Zhou, Sunhao Dai, Jun Xu
**Published:** 2026-10-21
**Summary:** LoopRec gives each item in a user's history its own adaptive recursion depth via a learned exit gate, beating fixed-depth sequential recommenders.

**What's new**
- Shows items in a sequence need vastly different computation; most converge shallow, a critical few need full depth.
- Oracle analysis proves non-uniform depth beats fixed depth, exposing wasted capacity and lost quality.
- LoopRec adds a lightweight binary exit-or-continue gate at each recursion boundary of a weight-shared block.
- Learns per-item depth without auxiliary labels and adapts to four backbone families.
- Learned allocation is interpretable: low-frequency items and recent interactions get deeper computation.

### [PhysiCalWorld: In-Context Physics Calibration for World Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30125v1)
**Authors:** Yunhao Luo, Wentao He, Yinpei Dai, Hyelin Nam, Hongze Fu, Joyce Chai, Nima Fazeli, Jeong Joon Park
**Published:** 2026-10-21
**Summary:** PhysiCalWorld calibrates action-conditioned world models to scene-specific physics via an in-context descriptor token from an exploratory interaction clip, without fine-tuning or physics labels.

**What's new**
- Encodes an exploratory interaction clip into a compact physics descriptor token for in-context calibration
- Trained end-to-end with latent prediction loss only, requiring no physics labels
- Descriptor extracted once at inference and reused across rollouts with negligible overhead
- Improves rollout accuracy and visual planning success across four physics-varied environments
- Learned descriptors form a physically structured latent space and generalize beyond training physics ranges

### [A Quasipolynomial Algorithm for $\ell_\infty$ Contractions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30122v1)
**Authors:** Ashkan Soleymani, Gabriele Farina
**Published:** 2026-10-21
**Summary:** Quasipolynomial-time algorithm computes eps-approximate fixed points of d-dimensional l-infinity nonexpansions, implying simple stochastic games are solvable in quasipolynomial time.

**What's new**
- First quasipolynomial algorithm for l-infinity nonexpansions, running in log(1/eps)^{O(log^2 d)} time.
- Core technique is a boosting argument that geometrically shrinks the search region.
- Implies simple stochastic games can be solved in quasipolynomial time.

### [Geometric Algebra for Neural Networks: A Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30121v1)
**Authors:** Tao Zhong, Kaison Fong, Keqin Wang, Christine Allen-Blanchette
**Published:** 2026-10-21
**Summary:** Survey of geometric algebra neural networks covering building blocks, equivariance, applications, performance, software, and failure modes, concluding they are not universally superior to tuned non-GA equivariant baselines.

**What's new**
- First comprehensive survey of geometric algebra neural networks, unifying building blocks, equivariance, and applications.
- Provides an algebra-selection guide and empirical comparison of GA-based vs other equivariant architectures.
- Includes failure-mode analysis: training pathologies, deployment constraints, and performance limitations.
- Finds strongest evidence for GA architectures in explicit geometric symmetries and heterogeneous geometric entities.
- Concludes GA networks are not universally superior; evidence for parameter efficiency and interpretability is limited.

### [Velocity Ambiguity Profiles: Time-Resolved Bayes-Risk Diagnostics for Flow Matching](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30107v1)

**Published:** 2026-10-21
**Summary:** Introduces Velocity Ambiguity Profile, a time-resolved Bayes-risk diagnostic that separates irreducible velocity ambiguity from reducible data-limited error in flow matching.

**What's new**
- Defines VAP as the time-local Bayes-risk floor of velocity prediction, decomposing loss into ambiguity plus reducible excess.

### [DiffVortex: A quantum-inspired fast solver for incompressible flows](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30012v1)
**Authors:** Yu Jun Loo, Silas Alben, Shravan Veerapaneni
**Published:** 2026-10-21
**Summary:** DiffVortex compresses the Biot-Savart kernel with QTT/MPO tensor networks, enabling differentiable GPU vortex-particle flow simulation with Lagrangian immersed bodies.

**What's new**
- Compresses the Biot-Savart kernel instead of the flow state, keeping Lagrangian vortex particles and bound vortex sheets for bodies.
- Proves a lifting theorem: QTT rank r of the kernel bounds the convolution MPO rank by 2^d r in d dimensions, independent of grid resolution.
- Uses quantics tensor cross interpolation to make the moment-to-local FMM stage a precomputed tensor-network contraction with quasi-linear complexity.
- PyTorch implementation is end-to-end differentiable, runs on GPUs, and handles 6.7e7 particles on a single device.
- Validated on an impulsively started cylinder and elliptical vortices, plus flows past moving, multiple, and deforming bodies with full-time gradients.
