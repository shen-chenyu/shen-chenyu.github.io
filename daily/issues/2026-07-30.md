### [Pass the Baton: Trajectory-Relayed On-Policy Distillation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26057v1)
**Authors:** Haolei Xu, Xiaowen Xu, Haiwen Hong, Zixuan Ni, Hongxing Li, Yiwen Qiu, Weiming Lu, Yongliang Shen
**Published:** 2026-07-28
**Summary:** Relay-OPD improves on-policy distillation by having the teacher briefly take over at detected failure points, boosting accuracy and cutting training length by over 50%.

**What's new**
- Identifies teacher-student continuation asymmetry on failed prefixes as a handoff trigger.
- Constructs relay trajectories where the teacher briefly takes over at trigger points.
- Concentrates limited relay budget on critical early positions to limit policy departure.
- Outperforms standard OPD by +5.73% and FastOPD by +1.49% on average for 1.7B model.
- Reduces training trajectory length by over 50% while achieving best or second-best results on all benchmarks.

### [$π\mathbf{R}^2$: Reactive Real-time Flow Policies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26055v1)
**Authors:** Sungjae Park, Shubham Tulsiani
**Published:** 2026-07-28
**Summary:** πR² makes action-chunking flow policies reactive and real-time by splitting conditioning into fast/slow channels and using latency-adaptive flow scheduling.

**What's new**
- Splits conditioning into fast proprioception and slow vision-language channels for reactivity within chunks.
- Introduces latency-adaptive flow schedule that treats in-flight actions as inpainting conditioning.
- Enables closed-loop replanning at ~25Hz (4x faster than base) on real hardware with minimal architecture changes.
- Improves success rate by up to 23% in simulation and 30% in real-world over baselines.

### [Spend Experts Where You Are Unsure: Confidence-Adaptive Routing for Mixture-of-Experts LoRA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26052v1)
**Authors:** Tom Saliencro, Rohan Desai, Priya Nair, Maya Lindqvist, Daniel Whitmore
**Published:** 2026-07-28
**Summary:** CARE adaptively routes tokens to varying numbers of experts based on confidence, improving efficiency and performance in MoE-LoRA.

**What's new**
- Introduces confidence-adaptive routing using router output distribution as uncertainty signal.
- Activates experts in nucleus fashion until cumulative weight threshold is met, with extension on disagreement.
- Budget thermostat calibrates threshold to match target average expert count.
- Drop-in, single-forward-pass rule with no extra parameters.
- Improves over fixed top-k MoE-LoRA at matched compute and enhances out-of-distribution detection.

### [Soft-mode nonlinearities away from ferroelectric phase transition](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26030v1)
**Authors:** Payel Shee, Ipek Efe, Jingwen Li, Kshitij V. Goyal, Morgan Trassin, Shovon Pal
**Published:** 2026-07-28
**Summary:** Study shows spontaneous polarization, not just local fields, is key for soft-mode nonlinearities in polar dielectrics.

**What's new**
- Isolated local-field effects from spontaneous polarization in paraelectric SrTiO3.
- Linear THz spectroscopy reveals soft mode is hybrid, driven by electronic polarizability.
- 2D THz spectroscopy shows system remains perturbative with robust local-field coherence.
- Microscopic model reproduces nonlinear signals, highlighting local fields' insufficiency alone.
- Spontaneous polarization is deterministic for soft-mode nonlinearities in polar dielectrics.

### [Accurate Prediction of the $α\to β$ Phase Transformation Temperature in Tin via Full Anharmonic Treatment](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25978v1)
**Authors:** Petr Šesták, Matous Mrovec, Martin Friák
**Published:** 2026-07-28
**Summary:** Full anharmonic treatment predicts the α→β tin transition temperature at 288 K, closely matching the experimental 286 K.

**What's new**
- ACE potential trained on DFT data enables accurate free energy evaluation.
- Quasi-harmonic approximation gives 377 K; full thermodynamic integration yields 288 K.
- Explicit anharmonicity is substantial for β-Sn but negligible for α-Sn.
- Anisotropic anharmonicity in β-Sn is confirmed by excess heat capacity and vibrational renormalization.
- Transition temperature accuracy is limited by underlying 0 K energetics.
