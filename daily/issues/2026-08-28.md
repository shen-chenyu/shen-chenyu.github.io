### [CritICL: Inference-Time Weak-to-Strong Generalization from Small Language Model Failure Modes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27455v1)
**Authors:** Yufan Wu, Yinghui He, Zhengyi Hu, Lang Wei, Ruichen Li, Qifan Yang, Ting Zhu
**Published:** 2026-08-27
**Summary:** CritICL uses failure modes from smaller models as critique-based in-context examples to improve LLM reasoning at inference time, matching test-time scaling with fewer tokens.

**What's new**
- Introduces CritICL, a framework that leverages weak model failure modes for inference-time guidance.
- Proposes two variants: CritICL-dynamic (input-specific) and CritICL-static (global profile).
- Outperforms standard ICL and matches test-time scaling methods with lower token cost.

### [TTPO: Test-Time Policy Optimization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27448v1)
**Authors:** Aozhe Wang, Zhengxi Lu, Jianze Wang, Shangke Lv, Ying Liu, Weiming Lu, Jun Xiao, Yueting Zhuang, Hua Yang, Qianglong Chen, Yongliang Shen
**Published:** 2026-08-27
**Summary:** TTPO is a label-free test-time training method that combines OPSD on agreeing rollouts and RL on disagreeing rollouts, matching supervised performance.

**What's new**
- Asymmetric objective: distill agreeing rollouts, penalize disagreeing ones
- Token-level selection refines both distillation and RL updates
- Majority-vote routing improves self-supervision as model improves
- Matches label-supervised OPSD on five competition benchmarks
- Raises Qwen3-1.7B from 38.0% to 45.2% in test-time training

### [Band's Geometry Origin of Quantum Spin Transport Phenomena](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27445v1)
**Authors:** Elena Derunova, Mazhar N. Ali
**Published:** 2026-08-27
**Summary:** A geometric framework links Fermi surface geometry to intrinsic spin transport, revealing hyperbolic regions and symplectic structures as origins of spin currents.

**What's new**
- Shows hyperbolic Fermi surface regions generate geometric spin-current responses.
- Relates spin operator algebra to exterior algebra of band tangent space under time-reversal symmetry.
- Introduces symplectic description of spin-separated transport on Fermi surfaces.
- Extends framework to 3D via contact geometry, connecting geometry to spin transport.

### [Grain Boundary Phase Transitions Enable Diffusionless Climb of Disconnections](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27314v1)

**Published:** 2026-08-27
**Summary:** Dislocation absorption at a grain boundary can drive disconnection climb without bulk diffusion, via localized GB phase transitions.

**What's new**

### [LeVJEPA: Efficient & Scalable Video Pretraining without the Heuristics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27395v1)

**Published:** 2026-08-27
**Summary:** LeVJEPA is a video pretraining method that removes heuristics like EMA and stop-gradient, achieving SOTA accuracy with up to 20.8x less compute.

**What's new**
- First video encoder trained with LeJEPA's collapse-free objective, using only an invariance loss and SIGReg regularization.
