# Daily Issue (JST): 2026-11-11

## What's New Today

**AI systems learn faster, safer, and more efficiently; materials simulation gets sharper**

Today's papers show AI research tightening its grip on efficiency and reliability: from trajectory-free molecular dynamics and adaptive quantization to robust preference optimization and deception mitigation. In materials and physics, machine-learned potentials and designed displacement schemes are making finite-temperature and defect simulations more accurate at lower cost. Across both domains, the theme is extracting more signal from fewer, noisier, or more constrained data.

**Highlights**
- KC-Maps learns SDE transition kernels without long trajectories, enabling fast molecular dynamics simulation.
- BAQ-TD preserves fast statistical rates for TD learning over rate-limited channels using adaptive quantization.
- Vendi Anomaly Scores detect anomalies via diversity change, avoiding density estimation and hyperparameters.
- WARP performs symmetry-constrained finite-temperature relaxations including anharmonicity and zero-point motion.
- SOTC-D designs atomic displacements for finite-temperature phonons from just eight force calculations.
- PORT achieves robust preference optimization via token-level reweighting under noisy annotations.
- e-PoG frames deception mitigation as an evaluation-side partially observable game, improving monitor robustness.

## Featured Papers

### [Kolmogorov-Chapman Maps: Trajectory-Free Learning for Simulating Molecular Dynamics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12880v1)
**Authors:** Majdi Hassan, Marta Skreta, Dominique Beaini, Kirill Neklyudov
**Published:** 2026-11-09
**Summary:** KC-Maps learns SDE transition kernels from trajectory-free marginal samples plus known drift/diffusion, enabling fast molecular dynamics simulation.

**What's new**
- Introduces Kolmogorov-Chapman Maps for learning SDE transition kernels without long simulated trajectories.
- Uses only trajectory-free marginal samples with known drift and diffusion coefficients.
- Enforces boundary conditions directly in the neural network architecture.
- Trains via a Kolmogorov-Chapman equation objective to recover correct dynamics.
- Outperforms classical molecular dynamics on peptide systems in GPU-hours.

### [Adaptive Quantization Preserves Fast Rates for TD Learning over Rate-Limited Channels](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12878v1)
**Authors:** Sreejeet Maity, Aritra Mitra
**Published:** 2026-11-09
**Summary:** BAQ-TD uses batching and adaptive quantization to preserve order-optimal TD convergence rates over rate-limited channels with exponentially lower communication cost.

**What's new**
- Proposes Batched Adaptively Quantized TD (BAQ-TD) for policy evaluation over finite-capacity channels.
- Uses batching to reduce variance of transmitted information and adaptive quantization to encode innovations.
- Proves a high-probability finite-time error bound for BAQ-TD.
- Shows O(d log T) bits over horizon T suffice for the order-optimal O~(1/T) rate.
- Achieves an exponential improvement in communication complexity over prior work.

### [Shortcuts and Boundaries: Verifiable RL Misalignment and Contextual Safety Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12865v1)
**Authors:** Xinjie Shen
**Published:** 2026-11-09
**Summary:** Verifiable reward-context conflicts show RL agents acquire profitable shortcuts from minority exposure, and contextual safety checks enable joint capability and compliance gains.

**What's new**
- Introduces verifiable reward-context conflicts where shortcuts out-earn all compliant strategies while compliance stays feasible.
- Encodes conflicts in task dynamics to scale diverse interactive environments and derive compliance feedback from execution histories.

### [Mitigating Agentic Deception via Evaluation-Side Partially Observable Games](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12835v1)
**Authors:** Hanxiao Li, Shutong Liu, Yongjia Ma, Jiaming Ji, Juntao Dai, Shuning Zhang, Kejian Shi, Kai Chen, Pu Feng, Yuqing Ma, Qi Dou, Yaodong Yang, Xianglong Liu, Simin Li
**Published:** 2026-11-09
**Summary:** e-PoG frames agent deception mitigation as an evaluation-side partially observable game with a monitor and verifier, achieving faithful equilibria and large gains in deception reduction and task success.

**What's new**
- Attributes agentic deception to evaluation-side partial observability: outcomes checked, not reasoning, tool use, or effects.
- Formulates a max-min extensive-form game (e-PoG) with agent, monitor, and fixed verifier, defining Subgame-Perfect Oversight Equilibrium.
- Proves SPOE existence and faithfulness with perfect verifiers, and robustness under imperfect verifiers that preserve monitor action rankings.
- Derives a two-timescale policy gradient algorithm, enhanced with NLI verification and deterministic checks.
- Reports 26.0% better deception mitigation and up to 39.8% higher task success on Agentic Role-Playing, SearchQA, and WebShop.

### [PORT: Preference Optimization via Robust Token-Level Reweighting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12763v1)

**Published:** 2026-11-09

**What's new**
- Moves robust preference optimization from sequence-level to fine-grained token-level reweighting.

### [Finite temperature and pressure structural relaxations including zero-point motion and anharmonicity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12736v1)
**Authors:** Henricus R. A. ten Eikelder, Florian Knoop, Florian Trybel
**Published:** 2026-11-09
**Summary:** WARP enables symmetry-constrained variable-cell crystal relaxations at finite temperature and pressure, including anharmonicity and zero-point motion.

**What's new**
- Introduces WARP, a Wyckoff-constrained anharmonic relaxation method using stochastic temperature-dependent effective potentials.
- Projects position updates onto Wyckoff positions and strains onto symmetric strains, so symmetry can only increase.
- Reproduces aluminum thermal expansion including the quantum plateau below 50 K.
- Matches phonon and path-integral equations of state for lanthanum hydrides at megabar pressures, relaxing LaH10 to the observed cubic phase.
- Captures brucite lattice parameters, bond distances, thermal expansion, and the O-D isotope effect versus neutron diffraction.

### [Designed atomic displacements for finite-temperature phonons from few force calculations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12218v1)
**Authors:** Albert Linda
**Published:** 2026-11-09
**Summary:** SOTC-D designs atomic displacements in supercells using a harmonic trial model to obtain finite-temperature phonons from far fewer force calculations than random sampling.

**What's new**
- Introduces special optimal thermal cells (SOTCs) with designed rather than random displacements for phonon fitting.
- Assigns each mode its quantum-thermal amplitude, optimizes signs, and pairs configurations with inverses to cancel cubic forces.
- Reproduces effective dispersions within 0.03-0.31 THz using eight force calculations across eleven crystals.
- Beats random sampling in all crystals at 4-16 calculations and sTDEP at eight after one trial update.
- Needs 2-16 times fewer calculations than stochastic SCHA in 13 of 14 model-potential cases.

### [Harmonic origin of local symmetry breaking in PbTe revealed using machine-learned potentials](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12598v1)
**Authors:** Seongjoo Jung, Nicola A. Spaldin
**Published:** 2026-11-09
**Summary:** Machine-learned potentials show PbTe's finite-range polar correlations arise from long-range harmonic interactions, not anharmonicity, with coherence beyond 50 Å at low T.

**What's new**
- Polar correlation range exceeds 50 Å at low temperature and shrinks as temperature rises.
- Crystal anharmonicity usually tied to polar nanoregions actually suppresses polar correlations.
- Long-range harmonic interactions extending to 13 Å are needed to reproduce dipole coherence.
- Extended harmonic interactions map to high curvature of the TO phonon branch at Γ.
- Comparison with NaCl links these interactions to local underscreening of Coulombic forces.
