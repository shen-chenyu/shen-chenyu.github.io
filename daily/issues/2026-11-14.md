**Published:** 2026-11-12
**Summary:** Mid-infrared pumping of NiO creates long-lived bimagnon correlations at finite momentum, decaying in 28 ps, over 100x longer than prior two-magnon dephasing times.

**What's new**
- First time-resolved RIXS observation of light-driven bimagnons at finite momentum in NiO

### [Quantum Boltzmann Equation for Static Correlation with Cumulant Kernel Closure](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17245v1)
**Authors:** Romit Chakraborty
**Published:** 2026-11-12
**Summary:** A parameter-free Quantum Boltzmann Equation with cumulant-kernel closure resolves strong static correlation at zero electronic temperature across diverse molecular test cases.

**What's new**
- Maps the BBGKY hierarchy to atomic-orbital matrix mechanics via Wigner-Weyl isomorphism for two-body cumulant scattering.
- Projects the two-body cumulant onto its lowest unitary invariant, using a single Coulomb-exchange contraction to avoid scaling bottlenecks.
