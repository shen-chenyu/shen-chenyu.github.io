# Daily Issue (JST): 2026-10-10

## What's New Today

**AI Safety, Robot Learning, and Materials Simulation Dominate Today's Preprints**

Today's papers span AI safety and agent reliability, robot learning from human data, and machine-learning-accelerated materials and physics simulation. Notable contributions include contextual safety filtering for motion generators, a statistical reassessment of AI time horizons, and benchmarks for agentic design and streaming perception. In materials, new methods tackle bifurcating systems, free-energy estimation, and universal interatomic potentials.

**Highlights**
- CSF introduces training-free contextual safety filtering for text-conditioned motion generators, grounding safety rules in reference trajectories.
- A statistical reanalysis of METR's AI time horizon finds the human-time-to-AI-difficulty mapping is nonlinear, with a flat region between 2 and 30 minutes.
- BrickBench evaluates agentic LEGO-set design, revealing that leading agents satisfy physical constraints but fall short of human creativity.
- Bi-FORK uses latent flow matching to model one-to-many solution maps in bifurcating physical systems like buckling beams and phase separation.
- FAITH presents a feasibility-aware safety-filtered RL framework for high-dimensional systems, amortizing minimal-intervention filtering with a neural network.
- VioLA learns generalist humanoid control policies from human motion data by predicting body and hand motion latents instead of joint commands.
- MERCE extrapolates correlation energies across electronic structure methods, achieving high-rank coupled cluster accuracy at reduced cost.

## Featured Papers

### [CSF: Contextual Safety Filtering for Motion Generators](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12467v1)
**Authors:** Lizhi Yang, Yiling Hou, Yao Tang, Junheng Li, Daniel Weng, Blake Werner, Aaron D. Ames
**Published:** 2026-10-08
**Summary:** CSF is a training-free safety filter that grounds natural-language safety rules in generator-produced reference trajectories to enforce scene-dependent safety for text-conditioned motion generators.

**What's new**
- Training-free contextual safety filtering for text-conditioned motion generators
- Grounds natural-language safety rules in safe/unsafe reference trajectories from the generator
- Uses affine safety values enforced by a safe reference tracking CBF-QP
- Activates intended rules in all explicit and scene-triggered unsafe cases across four generators
- Reduces danger-event rate up to 90% while preserving 88-100% of benign motions; shown on Unitree G1

### [On the estimation and validity of AI time horizons---a statistical look at the METR plot](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12466v1)
**Authors:** Drew T. Nguyen, William Fithian
**Published:** 2026-10-08
**Summary:** Spline and IRT models relax linearity in METR's AI time horizon, yielding better estimates and diagnostic plots for validity.

**What's new**
- Recomputed AI time horizons using splines and item-response theory on 228 tasks and 26 AIs.
- Found human-to-AI difficulty conversion is flat for 2-30 min but linear elsewhere.
- A 10x jump from 3 to 30 min is easier than from 30 min to 5 hours.
- New point estimates outperform under cross-validated proper scoring rules.
- Provided diagnostic plots to assess construct validity of time horizons.

**What's new**

### [Bi-FORK: Generative Modeling of High-Dimensional Bifurcating Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12449v1)
**Authors:** Anna Zimmel, Fleur Hendriks, Markus Holzleitner, Florian Sestak, Martin Weichselbaumer, Vlado Menkovski, Johannes Brandstetter
**Published:** 2026-10-08
**Summary:** Bi-FORK is a generative framework that learns one-to-many solution maps at symmetry-breaking bifurcations in high-dimensional physical systems.

**What's new**
- First generative framework for bifurcating systems in deep learning
- Uses latent flow matching to generate coherent full trajectories
- Repulsion-guided sampling recovers distinct solution branches in one pass
- Scales to discretizations up to 260,000 points, orders beyond prior work
- Validated on buckling beams, metamaterials, and Allen-Cahn phase separation

### [FAITH: Feasibility-Aware Safety-Filtered RL for High-Dimensional Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12432v1)
**Authors:** Songyuan Zhang, Baljeet Singh, Sarthak Ranjeet Kaingade, Chuchu Fan, Bryan Trinh
**Published:** 2026-10-08
**Summary:** FAITH is a model-free, feasibility-aware safety filter that learns state-action safety values and amortizes minimal-intervention filtering, enabling safe RL on high-dimensional and real humanoid systems.

**What's new**
- Separates safety from task policy by filtering actions at execution, avoiding competing updates.
- Learns optimal state-action safety value and amortizes minimal-intervention filtering with a feedforward network.
- Handles infeasible states by selecting actions with minimum predicted peak harm instead of undefined hard projections.
- Achieves highest return with no feasible-start violations on double integrator and Safety Gym.
- On 29-DoF humanoid, reaches 99.95% safety while retaining 97% return; demonstrated on real Unitree G1.

### [VioLA: Learning Generalist Humanoid Control Policies from Human Data](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12435v1)

**What's new**
- Realized a synthetic Kondo lattice interface between Weyl semimetal Eu2Ir2O7 and quantum-spin-ice candidate Tb2Ti2O7.

**What's new**
- First full theoretical description of the negatively charged CH_S^- defect in monolayer MoS2.
- Defect keeps similar geometry and singlet ground state across monolayer, bilayers, and MoS2/hBN.
