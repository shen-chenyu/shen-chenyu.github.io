### [In a Streaming World, Should You Stand Still? A Comprehensive Benchmark of Anomaly Detection in Streams](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.39215v1)
**Authors:** Magali Parrino, Antoine Ajenjo, Emmanuel Remy, Pierre Stephan, Pierre Senellart, Paul Boniol
**Published:** 2026-09-30
**Summary:** Large-scale benchmark shows static time series anomaly detection methods significantly outperform streaming approaches in most streaming settings, challenging common assumptions.

**What's new**

### [Low-Discrepancy Dither for Quantized Recurrent State Caches](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.39185v1)
**Authors:** Snigdha Chandan Khilar
**Published:** 2026-09-30
**Summary:** A deterministic golden-ratio Weyl dither beats stochastic rounding for quantized recurrent state caches, staying closer to full precision at no extra cost.

**What's new**
- Golden-ratio Weyl dither needs no random numbers and outperforms stochastic rounding for quantized recurrent state caches.
- Benefit holds across pure and hybrid models, storage formats, and long decoding horizons.
- Round-to-nearest discards small updates, so its error grows and it can look best only in short evaluations.
- A discrepancy analysis explains why Weyl dither ranks above stochastic rounding and round-to-nearest.

### [Do Self-Evolving Skills Generalize to Held-Out Tasks?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.39148v1)
**Authors:** Xihao Piao, Zifeng Wang, Zhen Chen
**Published:** 2026-09-30
**Summary:** Study finds self-evolving skills often fail to generalize to held-out tasks; proposes Generalizable Skill Optimization (GSO), which tops all six benchmarks.

**What's new**
- Of 21 skills improving on training tasks, only 5 keep all gains on test tasks, 13 keep some, 3 keep none.

**What's new**

### [Three-Dimensional Topology from Stacking-Controlled Umklapp Scattering in Large-Angle Twisted Graphite](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.39094v1)
**Authors:** Juncheng Li, Cong Chen, Wang Yao
**Published:** 2026-09-30
**Summary:** Stacking symmetry-inequivalent interfaces in large-angle twisted graphite yields nodal-line phases and a higher-order topological insulator, tunable by compression.

**What's new**
- Large-angle twisted graphene interfaces are governed by intervalley Umklapp tunneling, not local stacking.
- Competition of chiral and nonchiral tunneling produces one-ring and two-ring nodal-line phases.
- A C3-protected higher-order topological insulator appears with sublattice symmetry.
- DFT shows 21.8-degree twisted graphite is a higher-order topological insulator at equilibrium.
- Compression drives it into a topological Weyl semimetal phase.
