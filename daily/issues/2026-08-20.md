### [Multi-Agent AI System for Radiology Report Structuring and Quality Assurance with Independent Radiologist Evaluation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18072v1)

### [On the Fragility of Self-Improving Agents: Variance, Task Order, and Underspecification](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18066v1)

**Published:** 2026-08-18
**Summary:** Memory-based self-improving agents are fragile: performance varies across runs and task order, partly due to underspecification.

**What's new**
- Re-evaluates memory-based self-improving agents with multiple runs and shuffled task orders.
- Finds that self-improvement amplifies noise and is highly dependent on task order.

- Shows that even with better specification, performance gaps remain, indicating other factors.

### [TokEval: A Tokenizer Evaluation Suite](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18062v1)

**Published:** 2026-08-18

**What's new**
- Proposes metrics beyond fertility/compression, including UTF-8 boundary integrity and digit place-value alignment.
