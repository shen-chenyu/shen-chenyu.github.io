### [One Basis to Animate Them All: Gaussian Blendshape Distillation for Real-Time Avatars](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02207v1)
**Authors:** Ramazan Fazylov, Stamatis Lefkimmiatis, Ivan Laptev
**Published:** 2026-10-01
**Summary:** GALA distills pretrained 3D Gaussian avatars into identity-independent blendshapes with a shallow coefficient predictor, cutting CPU animation cost up to 1000x while keeping quality.

**What's new**
- Shows pretrained Gaussian avatar animation can be approximated by linear combinations of identity-independent blendshapes
- Introduces GALA, replacing per-frame neural decoding with a shallow MLP and linear blend
- Builds the basis via block-local PCA under a rendering-aware metric and memory budget
- Applies to multiple avatar architectures without retraining the original models
- Generalizes to held-out identities and reaches up to 60fps on mobile devices

### [KaliBench: A Fine-Grained Benchmark for Cybersecurity Tool Use on Kali Linux with Runtime-Free Verifiable Rewards](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02206v1)
**Authors:** Pengfei Li, Naufal Suryanto, Sicheng Zhang, Muzammal Naseer
**Published:** 2026-10-01
**Summary:** KaliBench benchmarks LLMs on natural-language-to-CLI translation for 1,642 Kali Linux tools, showing open-weight models struggle below 42% exact-command accuracy.

**What's new**
- First fine-grained benchmark for NL-to-CLI translation on Kali Linux: 8,504 query-command pairs across 1,642 tools.
- Covers 23 capability dimensions and 5 security phases with deterministic canonicalization and alias-aware evaluation.
- Multi-stage verification combines LLM validation, sandboxed execution, and human refinement for executability.
- Enables runtime-free verifiable rewards for training; SFT and RL boost an 8B model to match a 685B MoE.
- No open-weight model exceeds 42% exact-command accuracy in the unrestricted setting.

### [TACO: Ternary Absolute-max Column-wise One-sparse Optimizer for LLM Fine-Tuning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02199v1)
**Authors:** Jichao Jiang, Cristian McGee, El Houcine Bergou, Hanqin Cai, Aritra Dutta
**Published:** 2026-10-01
**Summary:** TACO is a ternary one-sparse optimizer that cuts LLM fine-tuning optimizer state by 174x versus AdamW8bit while matching accuracy and runtime.

**What's new**
- Computes exact steepest-descent direction under a dimension-normalized 1-to-1 operator norm.
- Selects the sign of the largest-magnitude entry per column of 2D weight matrices.
- Retains first-order gradients while making optimizer state memory nearly negligible.
- Reduces persistent optimizer state 174x (27.7 GB to 0.16 GB) and peak memory 2.9x on OPT-13B.
- Enables full-parameter fine-tuning of 30-32B models on a single 80 GB H100 GPU.

### [Cost-augmented Schrödinger bridges on graphs are exactly solvable: a Feynman-Kac tilt replaces learned control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02195v1)
**Authors:** Akshay Balsubramani
**Published:** 2026-10-01
**Summary:** Cost-augmented Schrödinger bridges on graphs are exactly solvable via a Feynman-Kac tilt, eliminating learned control and temporal-difference penalties.

**What's new**
- State cost folds into the reference process as a Feynman-Kac tilt, making the bridge a plain bridge.
- Bridge computed exactly by alternating two endpoint rescalings, each a sparse matrix-exponential application.
- No time discretization or learning needed; convergence rate depends only on endpoint coupling.
- Quadratic congestion cost yields strongly convex objective with residual bounding error.
- On protein folding, free-energy cost lowers expected barrier; scales to millions of intersections.

**Summary:** Multi-teacher on-policy distillation signals are shaped by loss averaging, Adam's first moment, BF16 rounding, and top-64 KL gradients, affecting task performance.

**What's new**

### [Every Ablation Is a Dose: Counterweights and the Semblance of Self-Repair](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02173v1)
**Authors:** Areeb Ahmad, Pratinav Seth, Vinay Kumar Sankarapu
**Published:** 2026-10-01
**Summary:** Self-repair in language models is reframed as a pre-existing counterweight gain, governed by an affine dose-response law.

**What's new**
- Ablation is treated as an uncalibrated dose on a signed contrast axis lambda.
- Causal repair follows an affine law E_r(lambda) = own_r + gamma_r * lambda.
- Slope gamma_r is a fixed coefficient predicting whether a unit counteracts or reinforces.
- 68 of 81 downstream directions across Gemma, Qwen, LLaMA, and Mistral follow the law.
- On GPT-2 Small IOI, seven of ten reachable heads follow the law and all are counterweights.
