# Daily Issue (JST): 2026-07-31

## What's New Today

**AI Agents Tackle Open-Ended Research, Accounting, and Security; Materials Physics Advances**

Today's papers span AI agent benchmarks for open-ended research and accounting, new defenses against backdoor attacks and jailbreaks, and fundamental advances in materials physics from magnetic hopfions at room temperature to ferroelectric switching.  The set also includes studies on LLM reasoning, world models, and the social dynamics of human-AI teams.

**Highlights**
- Shadow evaluations test if AI agents can conduct open-ended AI research, with frontier agents given 6 days and thousands of dollars of compute.
- APEX-Accounting benchmark reveals no frontier model passes more than 2.6% of real accounting tasks, highlighting a major capability gap.
- Magnetic hopfions, 3D topological solitons, are demonstrated at room temperature in a chiral magnet, opening doors for practical spintronic devices.
- A new framework (ba-occ-DFT) enables reliable defect calculations in narrow-gap semiconductors like InAs, circumventing the band gap problem.
- AgentSnare dynamically deploys decoy environments to mislead and divert autonomous penetration testing agents.
- MemSecBench tracks the lifecycle of memory poisoning in LLM agents, from persistence to consequence and repair.
- A compositional theory of causally masked transformers provides new expressivity bounds for finite-precision models.

### [When Do Learned Diffusion Proposals Help Constraint Solving? A Controlled Study on Continuous Algebraic Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27169v1)
**Authors:** Quang Bui, Sparsh Roy, Akash Gundimeda, Davin Yin
**Published:** 2026-07-29
**Summary:** Learned diffusion proposals for constraint solving only help in high-dimensional regimes where random multi-start fails, but classical multi-start solves all real-world benchmarks.

**What's new**
- MARC uses graph-neural diffusion denoiser to propose assignments, polished by exact computer-algebra descent and certified by symbolic checker.
- Random multi-start under same refinement budget sharply curtails the learned proposal's contribution on value decisions.
- Learned proposals beat random multi-start only in high dimension; advantage vanishes when variables couple.
- Best-of-K random multi-start success probability follows a simple formula with no free parameters (MAE 0.012).
- Across eight real-world systems, classical multi-start solved all, none in the learning-favorable regime.

### [Field Codes for Distributed Coupling Samplers and Certified Empirical Transport](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27078v1)

**What's new**
- Formulates three communication tasks for empirical optimal transport: coupling sampling, cost-evaluable output, and value-certified sampling.
