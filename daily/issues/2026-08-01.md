## What's New Today

**Daily Research Digest: AI Agents, Physics Simulations, and Scientific Discovery**

Today's papers span AI/ML advances in agentic systems, model efficiency, and evaluation, alongside significant progress in physics-based simulations and materials science. Key themes include improving LLM reliability and safety, developing more efficient training and inference methods, and leveraging AI for scientific discovery. Notable highlights include new benchmarks for computer-use agents, frameworks for trustworthy AI, and advances in quantum chemistry and materials modeling.

- New benchmarks and audits (OSReward, ORCA-bench, ClawTrack) reveal reliability gaps in computer-use and coding agents, with up to 15.3% incorrect failure verdicts.

- Scientific discovery: New materials insights include topological buffering in skyrmion crystals and negative thermal expansion in cubic ice.
- Agentic systems: Frameworks like MANTA and SKIMIX explore self-evolving multi-agent topologies and skill mixtures.
