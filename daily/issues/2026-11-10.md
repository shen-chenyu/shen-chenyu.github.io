## What's New Today

**LLM agents, neural PDE operators, and physics-informed ML dominate today's papers**

Today's batch spans LLM post-training and agent control, neural operators for coupled PDEs, and physics-informed machine learning for materials and quantum systems. ### [Keeping LLM Agents on Procedure: Executable Finite-State Control for Long-Horizon Instruction Fidelity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.10884v1)
**Authors:** Yutao Sun, Haokun Li, Mingshuai Chen, Guanyan Li, Zhongyi Wang, Shengchao Qin, Jianwei Yin
**Published:** 2026-11-07
**Summary:** An executable finite-state machine kept outside the model's context enforces procedural steps at tool calls, transitions, and turn end, sharply reducing long-horizon drift.

**What's new**
- FSM externalizes workflow state and enforces it at tool calls, state transitions, and turn end.
- On 200-account SOP-Bench batches, Haiku 4.5 with FSM averaged 139 correct accounts vs 48 for best baseline.
- Gains tracked how much plain agents drifted across three models, and held to the end of runs.
- Haiku 4.5 with FSM beat plain Sonnet 4.6 on procedural decisions at 38% of the cost.
- SWE-bench Verified, tau2-bench, and staged/cross-context workflows suggest execution control matters, not just stronger models.

### [CoupledFlow: One-Step Neural Operators for Coupled Multi-Physics PDEs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.10945v1)
**Authors:** Trong Khiem Tran, Minh Long Bui, Phi Le Nguyen, Mrinal Sen, Sanjay Srinivasan, Trong Nghia Hoang, Jana Doppa
**Published:** 2026-11-07
**Summary:** CoupledFlow is a neural operator framework using structure-constrained flow matching to achieve fast one-step prediction of coupled multi-physics PDE systems.

**What's new**
- First neural operator targeting coupled multi-physics PDEs via cross-process interaction modeling
- Structure-constrained flow matching learns process-specific mean velocity fields conditioned on full state
- Multi-physics mean-flow consistency identity defines a structure-constrained matching loss
- Posterior multi-output Gaussian processes sample correlated conditional interpolants for training
- Wasserstein-2 error bound proven in terms of matching loss and learned flow smoothness

### [The Surprising Effectiveness of Deleting Weights in LLM Post-Training](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.10866v1)
**Authors:** Jack Lu, Zhenbang Yang, Mike Lasby, Tejas Pote, Yani Ioannou, Mengye Ren
**Published:** 2026-11-07
**Summary:** Zeroing under 0.05% of LLM weights via a learned binary mask matches full fine-tuning across RL, SFT, and on-policy distillation while forgetting far less.

**What's new**
- Deletion Tuning learns a binary mask over transformer gate projections, deleting <0.05% of weights to match full fine-tuning.
- Matches FFT across RL with verifiable rewards, supervised fine-tuning, and on-policy distillation.
- Trained mask is hundreds to thousands of times smaller than an FFT checkpoint.
- Forgets far less: under RL, prior-task accuracy stays near base while FFT, LoRA, and random sparse tuning lose up to 5.4 points.
- Geometry analysis (stable rank, spectrum drift, principal-weight overlap) shows it is closest to FFT among sparse and LoRA baselines.

### [Imaging spin entanglement with a scanning tunneling microscope](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.10946v1)

**Published:** 2026-11-07

**What's new**

**What's new**
