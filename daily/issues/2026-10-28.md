# Daily Issue (JST): 2026-10-28

## What's New Today

**AI agents, KV-cache codecs, and materials ML dominate today's preprints**

Today's papers show a push toward more efficient and reliable AI systems: learned KV-cache compression, in-parameter memory, and agent harnesses that co-evolve with policies. In parallel, materials and physics work leans on machine learning for Hamiltonians, charge densities, and self-assembly dynamics, while several benchmarks probe the limits of LLM reasoning and verification.

**Highlights**
- DCKV learns an end-to-end transform codec for prompt KV caches, storing tokens in >55x fewer bytes than bf16 with minimal long-context loss.
- SKIM writes dialogue turns into frozen model parameters via self-generated questions, enabling recall without external memory.
- Evolving Agent Teams and CoHARL co-evolve agent policies with harnesses or team structures to escape local optima in coding and kernel tasks.
- Machine-learned Hamiltonians with polarizations address non-local electrostatic effects in heterogeneous systems.
- Charge-density-supervised GNNs improve atomistic energy prediction by learning real-space electronic structure.
- Benchmarks like PRISM-BENCH and DocXEval reveal grounding and explainability gaps in multimodal models beyond final-answer accuracy.
- Conservative dueling bandits and SetGRPO tackle safety and grader-noise issues in preference-based and rubric-based RL.

**Themes**
- AI: Efficient inference and memory (KV cache, in-parameter memory, test-time training)
- AI: Agentic systems and self-improvement (harnesses, co-evolution, routing)
- AI: Evaluation and reliability (benchmarks, verification, calibration)
- Materials: ML for electronic structure and dynamics (Hamiltonians, charge density, self-assembly)
- Physics: Quantum and transport phenomena (ARPES, spintronics, thermopower)

## Featured Papers

### [DCKV: Learned Transform Coding of the Prompt KV Cache in Hybrid Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36950v1)
**Authors:** Egor Maximov, Aleksei Merkelov, Vasiliy Alekseev, Ivan Krivorotov, Valentin Beliaev, Nikita Arsenin, Dmitry Bocharov, Aleksei Goncharov
**Published:** 2026-10-27
**Summary:** DCKV is the first end-to-end trained codec for prompt KV caches in hybrid language models, achieving 55-100x compression with small accuracy loss.

**What's new**
- First KV-cache codec trained end to end against a frozen language model
- Jointly optimizes transform, quantizer, and prompt-conditioned entropy models
- Entropy models conditioned on prompt tokens and already decoded neighbors
- On Qwen3.5-2B: >55x fewer bytes than bf16 with at most 1.8-point loss on three long-context benchmarks
- Over 100x compression at a loss of at most 3.6 points

### [Self-Keyed In-Parameter Memorization for External Memory-Free Dialogue Recall in Frozen Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36948v1)
**Authors:** XinYu Piao, Myunghoon Ryu, Joongheon Kim, Jong-Kook Kim
**Published:** 2026-10-27
**Summary:** SKIM stores dialogue turns directly in frozen model parameters using self-generated questions as retrieval keys, enabling recall without external memory.

**What's new**
- Closed-form method writes dialogue into model parameters, no external storage needed
- Model generates its own varied questions per turn as keys for later retrieval
- Multiple question variants let paraphrased user queries still match stored turns
- Evaluated on five language models; recalls most stored turns from later questions

### [Evolving Agent Teams](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36848v1)
**Authors:** Shiyi Cao, Ziming Mao, Dacheng Li, Joseph E. Gonzalez, Ion Stoica
**Published:** 2026-10-27
**Summary:** Evolving Agent Teams is a multi-agent framework with a supervisor that diagnoses local optima and restructures agent teams, using a persistent file system to accumulate experience.

**What's new**
- Central supervisor diagnoses each local optimum and restructures the agent team accordingly
- Persistent file system stores a learning document, evolution history, and per-team artifacts
- Generates a CUDA kernel with 1.2x mean speedup over FlashInfer MLA paged decode from scratch
- Wins on 36 of 47 FlashInfer-Bench production workloads under extended token budget
- Also improves KDA prefill, MoE expert-offload serving, and RoboDojo robot manipulation

### [CoHARL: Co-Evolving Agent Policies and Executable Harnesses with Reinforcement Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36469v1)
**Authors:** Jianwen Chen, Peng Xia, Kaide Zeng, Siwei Han, Zhen-Yu Zhang, Charles Fleming, Xinyu Ye, Huaxiu Yao
**Published:** 2026-10-27
**Summary:** CoHARL co-evolves an LLM agent policy with a population of executable harnesses via population-based RL, reaching 27.6% resolved rate on SWE-rebench.

**What's new**
- Co-evolves a shared policy and a population of executable harnesses during training instead of fixing one side.
- A proposer revises harness code using the policy's execution traces as learning progresses.
- Achieves 27.6% mean resolved rate on SWE-rebench, +6.4 points over harness-only search.
- Shows gains on SWE-bench Verified and Terminal-Bench 2.0 under matched training budgets.
- Outperforms one-time harness search before RL and alternating policy updates with single-harness revisions.

### [PRISM-BENCH: Diagnosing Grounded and Faithful Multimodal Reasoning Beyond Final-Answer Accuracy](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36554v1)
**Authors:** Vansh Bhatia, Mehdi Rezagholizadeh, Parsa Ashrafi Fashi, Igor Gilitschenski, Vikram Appia, Emad Barsoum
**Published:** 2026-10-27
**Summary:** PRISM-BENCH is a 1,000-question benchmark with expert reasoning traces that diagnoses grounding, faithfulness, and efficiency in vision-language models beyond final-answer accuracy.

**What's new**
- Introduces 1,000 expert-curated questions across five under-represented reasoning categories with validated idealized reasoning traces.
- Perception probe attributes ~85% of frontier-model failures to misperception, locating the bottleneck at grounding.
- Trace alignment predicts answer correctness across all 25 systems (macro AUROC 0.66) and survives length control.
- CORE, an efficiency-aware metric, reorders 24.2% of model pairs versus accuracy and is uncorrelated with response length.
- Best accuracy is 66.0%; top systems trail humans by 18.4 points, with frontier gains in recognition not spatial grounding.

### [Conservative Dueling Bandits: Preference Learning under Baseline Constraints](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36708v1)
**Authors:** Rohan Deb, Mohammad Ghavamzadeh, Arindam Banerjee
**Published:** 2026-10-27
**Summary:** Conservative contextual dueling bandits keep the better displayed item's cumulative score above a fraction of a trusted baseline's, with new algorithms MaxInP-BC and BTIGW-BC.

**What's new**
- Formalizes conservative contextual dueling bandits where the constraint depends only on utility differences, needing no absolute rewards.
- Shows transferring CLUCB's safety test unchanged can incur linear regret even with known baseline utilities.
- MaxInP-BC certifies scores via utility gaps and pairs the baseline with an informative partner during fallbacks.
- MaxInP-BC is safe with high probability and has regret O~(d√T/κ) plus horizon-independent conservatism cost.
- BTIGW-BC converts an online logistic-regression oracle's guarantee into cumulative safety certificates with finitely many fallbacks.

### [SetGRPO: Calibrating Grader-Induced Policy Optimization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36807v1)
**Authors:** Jiale Huang, Zixu Li, Zhiwei Chen, Zhiheng Fu, Jinhe Bi, Yupeng Hu
**Published:** 2026-10-27
**Summary:** SetGRPO calibrates grader-induced uncertainty in rubric-based RL, controlling group coefficient shifts and direction-reversal risk while preserving the GRPO objective.

**What's new**
- Identifies that grader randomness shifts entire GRPO group coefficients, not just individual scores, causing policy-dependent update changes.
- Joint Reward Set Calibration builds an uncertainty set for training coefficients under exchangeability.
- Geometry-Guided Safe Recovery maps coefficients into local update geometry to find safe recovery ratios per group.
- Selective Risk-Gated Optimization uses a calibrated global threshold to bound conditional direction-reversal risk.
- Achieves highest macro average on ten open-ended benchmarks across Qwen3.5 and Llama3.2 families.
