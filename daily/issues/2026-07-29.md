# Daily Issue (JST): 2026-07-29

## What's New Today

**Frontier Models, Physics, and Agents: Scaling, Safety, and Scientific Discovery**

Today's papers span foundational AI advances, including the 2.8T-parameter Kimi K3 MoE model and new theoretical results for diffusion models and PDE solvers, alongside practical work on agentic systems, retrieval-augmented generation, and scientific applications from materials science to quantum computing. Key themes include the tension between model scale and efficiency, the need for robust evaluation and safety mechanisms, and the growing role of AI in autonomous research and physical simulation.

**Highlights**
- Kimi K3 (2.8T params, 104B active) achieves ~2.5x scaling efficiency over K2 with novel attention and MoE designs.

- Agentic systems advance with falsifiable commitment planning (FCPAgent) and a unified framework for autonomous research efficiency (Efficiency Matters).

- Efficient inference advances: MXFP4 attention quantization, page-local KV cache summaries (LOCKS), and expansion-aware speculative decoding for MoE (DraftExpert).

**What's new**
