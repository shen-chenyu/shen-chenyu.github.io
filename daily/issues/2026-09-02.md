### [Context-Aware Interleaved Batching for WhisperX](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.31170v1)
**Authors:** Carlos Bain, Max Bain

### [Strain-Tunable Spin Relaxation in Germanium from First Principles](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.31169v1)
**Authors:** Lauren A. Tan, Shaelyn Iyer, Ivan Maliyov, Jinsoo Park, Marco Bernardi
**Published:** 2026-08-31
**Summary:** First-principles study predicts strain-tunable spin relaxation in germanium, showing compressive biaxial strain can enhance hole spin lifetime by up to two orders of magnitude.

**What's new**
- First fully ab initio prediction of carrier mobilities and spin relaxation times in bulk Ge, matching experiments over 100-400 K.
- Identifies distinct microscopic mechanisms for spin vs. momentum relaxation via valley- and phonon-resolved spin-flip scattering.
- Shows compressive biaxial strain (up to 5%) enhances hole spin lifetime by ~100x via valence-band splitting and reduced spin mixing.
- Provides practical route for engineering long-lived hole spins in Ge-on-Si quantum devices.

### [Constant Individual Regret in General Games](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.31166v1)
**Authors:** Mingyang Liu, Gabriele Farina, Asuman Ozdaglar
**Published:** 2026-08-31
**Summary:** Introduces ECHO-OFTRL, a deterministic uncoupled algorithm achieving constant individual regret in finite normal-form games.

**What's new**
- Removes polylogarithmic horizon dependence for individual regret in general games.
- Combines OFTRL with an EMA cascade for high-order optimism.
- Guarantees regret bounded by O(poly(N, log m_max)) for all horizons.
- Fully uncoupled and deterministic, using full-information feedback.
- Draws inspiration from modern filter design for optimism.

### [Overcoming critical slowing down in frustrated spin systems by learned multiscale sampling](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.31114v1)
**Authors:** Gabriele Bandini, Giulio Biroli, Patrick Charbonneau, Andrea Gambassi
**Published:** 2026-08-31
**Summary:** Learned multiscale sampling (WCRG) overcomes critical slowing down in frustrated spin systems where cluster algorithms fail, achieving O(log L) complexity.

**What's new**
- WCRG learns cluster distributions via wavelets instead of constructing them, bypassing frustration limitations.
- Achieves O(log2 L) sampling complexity at Ising-like critical points, far outperforming local MCMC.
- Reproduces key statistical properties across phases, including local-field distribution and structure factor.
- Identifies expressiveness of energy-based models as key tradeoff for sampling accuracy.
