### [Stochastic Dynamics on Persistence Diagram Space via Reinforcement Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06276v1)
**Authors:** Farzana Nasrin
**Published:** 2026-08-06
**Summary:** Introduces a reinforcement learning framework for stochastic dynamics on persistence diagram space, enabling probabilistic modeling and adaptive topological simplification.

**What's new**
- Models PD evolution as controlled Markov processes with variable cardinality.
- Proves geometric ergodicity and unique stationary laws for the induced chains.
- Uses RL to guide dynamics toward distributional, task-specific, and compression objectives.
- Demonstrates structure-preserving complexity reduction on synthetic and neuroimaging data.

### [NestyNet. II. Coherent Function-Space Posteriors from Scientific Neural Surrogates (or How to Avoid Expensive MCMC)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06274v1)
**Authors:** Rodrigo Ibata, Wassim Tenachi, Foivos Diakogiannis, Neil Ibata, Anirudh Shankar
**Published:** 2026-08-06
**Summary:** A method to obtain low-dimensional, coherent function-space posteriors for neural surrogates, avoiding expensive MCMC.

**What's new**
- Linearizes fitting to map measurement perturbations to function perturbations via converged Jacobians and Gauss-Newton curvature.
- Leading singular functions define coherent deformation modes, enabling efficient posterior sampling.
- Distinguishes repeated-experiment covariance from local Laplace posterior.
- Demonstrates on an 800-parameter stellar disk fit, capturing 99% of vertical-force variance with 4 coordinates.
- Propagates 4000 draws through derivative-dependent quantities in 0.8s.

### [The Illusion of Visual Tool-Use: A Causal Audit of Thinking with Images](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06270v1)
**Authors:** Zhiheng Wang, Bo Peng, Lai Wei, Chaochao Lu
**Published:** 2026-08-06
**Summary:** Visual tool-use in multimodal LLMs often yields marginal gains; causal audit reveals it's not broadly effective, only for a calibrated minority.

**What's new**
- Formulates visual tool-use as a causal graph with observation-mediated paths and action-induced shortcuts.
- Introduces Visual Evidence Gain, a step-level estimand to isolate each observation's causal contribution.
- Identifies two failure modes: Calling Without Looking and Looking Without Planning.
- Shows aggregate accuracy gains are concentrated in a small calibrated subset, termed the illusion of visual tool-use.

### [Hypothesis Testing with Conditional Queries: Learnability and the Value of Interaction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06262v1)
**Authors:** Zonghuan Xu
**Published:** 2026-08-06
**Summary:** This paper studies adaptive vs non-adaptive hypothesis testing with conditional queries, showing learnability depends on separation and interaction yields a quadratic query advantage.

**What's new**
- Learnability iff positive separation in pairwise conditional probabilities; otherwise error is 1/2 at any finite budget.
- Constructs randomized non-adaptive tester using O(N^2(T + log(1/ρ))) queries that matches adaptive transcript within ρ in TV distance.
- Shows a matching lower bound: constant adaptive queries vs Ω_ε(N^2) non-adaptive queries, giving a Θ_ε(N^2) adaptivity gap.
