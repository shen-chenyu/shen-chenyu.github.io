## Featured Papers

### [Spectrum Adaptation of AI-Native Autoencoder Communications with Transfer Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34942v1)
**Authors:** Yalin E. Sagduyu, Tugba Erpek
**Published:** 2026-11-25
**Summary:** Transfer learning fine-tunes only selected AE layers to adapt AWGN-trained autoencoder communications to Rayleigh fading and front-end impairments, cutting BER and cost.

**What's new**
- Shows AWGN-trained autoencoders degrade severely under Rayleigh fading with practical front-end impairments.
- Proposes low-latency transfer learning that fine-tunes a small subset of encoder/decoder layers while freezing the rest.
- Formulates layer selection as a budget-constrained optimization to pick the most informative parameters.
- Partial updates remove the mismatch-induced error floor, approaching BER of fully retrained models.
- Cuts training time, MAC operations, energy, and updated-parameter fraction with modest adaptation.

### [Tokenisation is not in APX](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34938v1)
**Authors:** Violeta Kastreva, Philip Whittington, Dennis Komm, Tiago Pimentel
**Published:** 2026-11-25
**Summary:** Proves tokenisation is not in APX: unless P=NP, no polynomial-time algorithm can approximate the optimal tokeniser within any constant factor.

**What's new**
- Tokenisation is shown to be outside APX for direct and bottom-up methods under compression or unigram log-likelihood objectives.
- Result holds for both binary and larger alphabets.
- Even over unary alphabets, direct tokenisation is APX-hard, ruling out a polynomial-time approximation scheme.

### [Can image generation help visual reasoning? An empirical study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34935v1)
**Authors:** Xueyang Yu, Yifan Jing, Taiming Lu, Linrong Cai, Zhipeng Cai, Yunyang Xiong, Gabriel Sarch, Zhuang Liu
**Published:** 2026-11-25
**Summary:** Testing whether generated helper images improve visual question answering, the study finds generation lowers mean accuracy by 3.7 points across 13 model-generator pairs.

**What's new**
- Generation reduced mean accuracy by 3.7 percentage points, with declines on nine of ten tasks.
- Unified models BAGEL and Janus-Pro-7B each lost accuracy on eight tasks.
- On VSTAR, adding a generated image to the same planning hint cut accuracy by 10.47 points.
- Repeated image edits produced mixed results: ChartQAPro and MathVista stayed below baselines, while ERQA gained three points after ten edits.

### [Diverse Alternative Reasoning Traces for Hard Math Problems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34928v1)
**Authors:** Lukas Aichberger, Adam Golinski, Arno Blaas, Arwen Bradley, Jacob Springer, Mehrdad Farajtabar, Michael Kirchhof, Sinead Williamson
**Published:** 2026-11-25
**Summary:** DART mines rejected reasoning traces by decomposing them, ranking steps by position-weighted uncertainty, and generating distinct alternatives, recovering 37% more hard math problems than Monte Carlo sampling.

**What's new**
- Starts from rejected traces instead of accepted ones, decomposing them into reasoning steps for targeted repair.
- Ranks steps by position-weighted uncertainty to focus alternative generation where it matters most.
- Generates deliberately distinct alternatives for top-ranked steps using reference-answer guidance and a meta-thought.
- Recovers 37% more greedy-unsolved DeepMath-103K problems than MC sampling at matched trace counts.
- Post-training Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct on DART traces yields best point estimate in five of six MATH-500/AIME evaluations.

### [Mixture-of-Loops Transformer](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34923v1)
**Authors:** Lianghui Zhu, Shijie Wang, Xinggang Wang, Jinze Bai, Bo Wang, Xiang Bai
**Published:** 2026-11-25
**Summary:** MoLT uses keys and values as memory selectively reused across loops and depths, with U-shaped variants improving efficiency and quality over OLMo3 in pretraining.

**What's new**
- Mixture-of-Loops Attention (MoLA) treats KV as memory for selective reuse across depths and loops.
- MoLT-KV restarts from embeddings and reads current-loop local KV plus preceding-loop memory KV for efficiency.
- Loop forcing enables parallel training and single-traversal MoL decoding beyond the training loop horizon.
- U-shaped MoLT separates memory construction from prediction readout and skips unused decoder traversals.
- U-MoLT beats OLMo3 at 700M/1.5B dense and 3B MoE, with 1.7x CE token efficiency at 700M.

### [How Much Do Coding Agents Over-Edit, and Why? An Empirical Study of Unneeded Edits in Repository-Level Agent Patches](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34875v1)
**Authors:** Aakash Goel, Benjamin Steenhoek, Yu Hu
**Published:** 2026-11-25
**Summary:** Study of 7,660 agent trajectories finds agent patches add mostly tests/docs, and their code is on average at least as necessary as developers'.

**What's new**
- Introduces Delta Residue Attribution (DRA) to trace and revert patch units, rerunning tests to judge necessity.

- DRA pruning with visible tests removes most unneeded code (67% recall, 46% precision) while 89% of pruned patches still resolve.

### [LoopTitan: What Does Parameter Revisit Buy a Mixture-of-Experts?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34821v1)
**Authors:** Zichen Tian, Yudong Lu, Xuansheng Wu, Na Zou, Xia Hu, Guanchu Wang, Qianru Sun
**Published:** 2026-11-25
**Summary:** Parameter revisit in looped MoE buys almost nothing; gains come from network shape, residual scale, and added compute that standard MoE can adopt.

**What's new**
- Matched non-revisit controls retain all reported looped MoE gains, showing revisit itself contributes almost nothing.
- Most gain traced to network shape; residual scale acts as lower learning rate on revisited layers, vanishing under Muon.
- One extra visit beats doubling experts, but deeper standard MoE under Muon captures 90.2% of that gain.
- Proposes LoopTitan-Base, a stronger MoE baseline matching top looped recipes without revisit or residual scale.
- Releases LoopTitan and 17 pretrained models with pre-registered predictions and public experiment history.

### [Guidance as Variance Matching](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34801v1)

**Published:** 2026-11-25
**Summary:** Guidance works by restoring the variance lost in posterior-mean predictions, and a closed-form variance-matching rule recovers hand-tuned guidance scales across eleven models.

**What's new**
- Guidance worsens posterior-mean prediction yet improves samples, contradicting the assumption it improves the mean.
- Variance-matched guidance picks the scale by matching guided prediction variance to data variance, with no tuning.
- It recovers hand-picked scales of eleven models across image, video, audio, motion, and world generation.
- Per-noise-level variance matching yields a schedule users prefer to hand-crafted ones on SD 3.5 Medium.
- Guidance rescales model error differences into samples with the posterior covariance, implying negative scales can work.
