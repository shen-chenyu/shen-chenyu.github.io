### [SPADE: Self-Play in Adaptive Synthetic Executable Environments](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19197v1)
**Authors:** Bo Liu, Simon Yu, Yiding Jiang, Ao Qu, Andrew Zhao, Zichen Liu, Junsu Kim, Zijian Zhou, Seungone Kim, Tongzheng Ren, Mickel Liu, Hanfei Yu, Zhaorun Chen, Weiyan Shi, Paul Pu Liang, Luke Zettlemoyer, Yejin Choi, Natasha Jaques
**Published:** 2026-08-19
**Summary:** SPADE is a self-play RL framework where an LLM designs executable environments and another learns in them, improving generalization across benchmarks.

**What's new**
- Introduces a self-play RL framework with an Environment Designer and Reasoning Agent.
- Uses regret-based signals to generate environments at the edge of agent capabilities.
- Grounds environment design on pretraining corpus documents and uses environment memory.
- Improves over fixed-environment baselines by +5.3 on eight benchmarks and +13.9 on ACEBench-Agent.

### [ADEPT: Accelerating Dexterity via Pre-Training and Post-Training using Reinforcement Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19182v1)
**Authors:** Jayjun Lee, Jessica Yin, Asif Rana, Nicholas Blauch, Sam Mady, Mohak Bhardwaj, Nima Fazeli, Nathan Ratliff, Karl Van Wyk, Ankur Handa
**Published:** 2026-08-19
**Summary:** ADEPT is a large-scale RL framework that pre-trains dexterous policies on generic reposing tasks and post-trains them for sim-to-real transfer, enabling long-horizon manipulation on high-DoF robots.

**What's new**
- Pre-training on a generic object reposing task provides a reusable prior for downstream dexterous manipulation.
- A stable post-training recipe (behavior-cloning distillation, critic warm-up, conservative updates) prevents degradation of pre-trained skills.
- Joint-space Geometric Fabric mediates between RL policy and robot, enabling safe exploitation of full kinematic dexterity.
- Zero-shot sim-to-real transfer on 23-DoF Kuka-Allegro and 29-DoF Flexiv-Sharpa with raw visuo-tactile perception.
- Achieves human-level speed on long-horizon tasks from challenging initial states.

### [Beyond Teacher Likelihood: Group-Calibrated On-Policy Distillation for Long-Context Reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19181v1)
**Authors:** Zhu Zhang, Jixun Wang, Xiaoang Xu, Xiaorong Wang, Zihan Zhou, Zhiyuan Wang, Shuo Wang, Chaojun Xiao, Yuezhi Zhou
**Published:** 2026-08-19
**Summary:** Introduces Group-Calibrated On-Policy Distillation (GC-OPD) to align teacher token-level guidance with verifier rewards, improving long-context reasoning.

**What's new**
- Identifies teacher-verifier disagreement in long-context OPD, worsening with longer inputs.
- Proposes group-relative normalization of verifier rewards and OPD scores to compute signed disagreement residual.
- Uses relative-advantage-based credit assignment to distribute trajectory-level residual across tokens.
- GC-OPD boosts Qwen3-4B/8B averages by ~11.4 and ~9.5 points over baselines on five benchmarks.

### [Lévy Attention: Single-Pass Predictive Uncertainty for Continuous-Time Attention](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19171v1)
**Authors:** Sotirios P. Chatzis, Loukas Papadoulas
**Published:** 2026-08-19
**Summary:** Lévy Attention is a stochastic cross-attention operator that provides closed-form predictive uncertainty for continuous-time attention at no extra cost.

**What's new**
- Introduces a Poisson random measure-based attention that outputs a stochastic integral, replacing softmax with a mollified cosine kernel.
- Provides exact closed-form variance identity for uncertainty, combining evidence and value spread without a trained head.
- Shows uncertainty signal improves over MC dropout and achieves better CRPS with a single pass.
- Demonstrates negligible accuracy loss on t-PatchGNN and fast uncertainty ranking on unseen patients.

### [Grouping the Stochastic Machine: Precision, Not Capability, as the Frontier Metric for AI Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19140v1)
**Authors:** George Andrikopoulos
**Published:** 2026-08-19
**Summary:** Argues precision, not capability, is the key metric for frontier AI systems, proposing a cheap, deterministic grouping measurement to guide improvements.

**What's new**
- Introduces precision as the frontier differentiator, measured by output spread across repeated identical requests.
- Proposes a cheap, deterministic grouping metric using fixed tasks and temperature, without model-in-the-loop grading.
- Distinguishes consistent failures (correctable by rule adjustments) from scattered failures (requiring model changes).
- Demonstrates the method with a real run: a rule closed a gap from 0/5 to 5/5, but rule-derived tasks showed no value.

### [JANUS: A Multi-modal Foundation Neural Sampler for Disordered Materials](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19116v1)

**Published:** 2026-08-19

### [A single design choice determines whether machine learning models of materials make physically impossible predictions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18714v1)
**Authors:** Can Polat, Mustafa Kurban, Erchin Serpedin, Hasan Kurban
**Published:** 2026-08-19
**Summary:** A single design choice—whether features carry parity labels—determines if ML models of materials make physically impossible predictions, as shown by a group-theoretic criterion.

**What's new**
- Introduces the 'parity gap' criterion, computed from group theory, to identify exposed properties and crystals.
- Shows parity-labeled models predict zero piezoelectric tensors for centrosymmetric crystals, while rotation-only models fail on 90-96% of cases.
- Demonstrates that training on explicit zeros does not restore exactness, and frozen backbones inherit symmetry limitations.
- Provides a quick verification method: one reflection at random initialization confirms the label in seconds.

### [Molecular Insights into Gas Nanofilms Confined Between Bulk Liquid Phases](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18891v1)
**Authors:** Yafan Yang, Zufeng Zuo, Xingyu Zhao, Shuyu Sun, Denvid Lau
**Published:** 2026-08-19
**Summary:** Molecular dynamics simulations reveal that gas nanofilms between liquids exhibit thickness-dependent surface tension, negative disjoining pressure, and morphological transitions to bubbles.

**What's new**
- Surface tension decreases exponentially with film thickness, with increasing negative disjoining pressure.
- Thinning induces a transition from planar film to spherical bubble via liquid bridge formation.
- Larger film areas amplify thickness dependence and deviations from classical density functional theory.
- Discrepancies with cDFT are attributed to thermal capillary-wave fluctuations in MD.
- Disjoining pressure magnitude increases with decreasing temperature, opposite to liquid nanofilms.
