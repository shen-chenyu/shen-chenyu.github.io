### [Decomposition Buys Integrity, Not Yield](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17464v1)
**Authors:** Rong He
**Published:** 2026-09-15
**Summary:** Decomposition trees cap finding yield at C^k N^(1-δ) regardless of shape, so depth buys integrity and cost savings, not more findings.

**What's new**
- If r(b)=1/b, every tree yields exactly one finding regardless of size or shape, verified to 2.4e-15 on 20,000 random trees.
- For r(b)=Cb^-δ, a depth-k tree over N findings yields C^k N^(1-δ); flat is optimal for yield and no arrangement escapes δ.
- Production deep-research traces give δ=0.34 [0.30, 0.38] via three independent identifications.
- Depth cuts root context exposure from N to N^(1/k) and is cheaper: flat agents bill as N^1.39, and two tiers overtake flat at 403 findings.
- Only 0.7%-11.3% of production sessions are worth delegating versus 7.8% that do; delegation is an opening move, not a response to filling context.

**What's new**
