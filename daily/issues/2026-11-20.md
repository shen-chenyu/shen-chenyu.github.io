### [Why Do LLMs Help with Scientific Misconduct Requests and What to Do About It?: Increasing the Trustworthiness of AI for Science](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25492v1)
**Authors:** Almene De Meran Meguimtsop, Daniel E. Acuna
**Published:** 2026-11-19
**Summary:** LLMs recognize scientific misconduct requests (91%) but refuse only 34%, and simple prompt fixes raise refusal rates to about 87%.

**What's new**
- Models identify misconduct requests even in covert cases (91%) yet comply most of the time, refusing only 34%.
- Rephrasing requests as questions about permissibility instead of instructions raised refusal from 52% to 88%.
- Asking models to assess the request before responding raised refusal on adversarial prompts from 58% to 87%.
- Reminding models of responsible research conduct principles raised refusal on adversarial prompts to 86%.
- Study spans 8 LLMs and 1,350 prompts from the SciIntBench research integrity benchmark.

### [Origin and hole-density dependence of the intrinsic spin Hall conductivity in inorganic halide perovskites](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25488v1)
**Authors:** Thanh Tung Huynh, Yuefeng Yin, Weiyao Zhao, Junlin Yan, Qurat Ul Ain, Jacek J. Jasieniak, Julie Karel, Nikhil V. Medhekar
**Published:** 2026-11-19
**Summary:** First-principles study shows intrinsic spin Hall conductivity in CsBX3 perovskites comes from halide-p spin Berry curvature and is activated by hole doping, not atomic spin-orbit strength.

**What's new**
- Identifies spin Berry curvature of spin-orbit-split halide-p bands 1.1-2.6 eV below VBM as the source of intrinsic spin Hall conductivity.
- Undoped centrosymmetric perovskites show cancelled response because the VBM is an s-p singlet shifted only in second order.
- Hole doping recovers the response; at EDL-reachable densities CsPbI3 matches p-GaAs spin Hall conductivity.
- Response depends on halide-p band position relative to VBM, not atomic spin-orbit constant, a criterion for any orbital-singlet band-edge semiconductor.
- Proposes CsPbI3 as a gate-controlled spin-current source for perovskite spin-orbit torque devices.

### [WeightCode: Installing New Capabilities into Neural Networks via Weight-Space Programming](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25476v1)
**Authors:** Bo Li, Taoyong Cui, Bob Zhang, Mengran Li, Chengyang Zhang, Yang Zhang, Pheng Ann Heng, Wanli Ouyang, Zhenfei Yin, Yingcheng Wu, Ling Yang
**Published:** 2026-11-19
**Summary:** WeightCode uses a frozen language model to write executable weight programs that install new capabilities into trained networks without gradient training.

**What's new**
- Introduces Weight-Space Programming (WSP): executable programs that read, construct, or modify network weights directly.
- Installs unseen command compositions (0% to 99.8% exact match) and a new pickup action (0% to 98.4% success) without gradient training.
- On ImageNet, four weight programs yield net gains of 8.84-17.62 points while preserving clean accuracy, beating eight fine-tuning methods.
- Causal interventions show installed noise-correction gates drive robustness gains, with selective activation preserving clean accuracy.
- Weight programs transfer across five independently trained ViTs, retaining 83-110% of source-network gains without further LLM calls.

### [FamilySpace: From Emergent Chemical Semantics to Controllable Crystal Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25452v1)
**Authors:** Haowei Hua, Irwin King, Wanyu Lin
**Published:** 2026-11-19
**Summary:** FamilySpace controls elemental-family composition in crystal generation by exploiting emergent chemical semantics in a vanilla VAE's atom-wise latent space.

**What's new**
- Shows vanilla crystal VAEs implicitly organize atom-wise latents by elemental family, termed emergent chemical semantics.
- Proposes FamilySpace: a family-indexed multi-source flow model trained on family-matched source-target pairs.
- User-specified family templates select source components per atom, enabling direct control of family composition.
- On MP-20 and MPTS-52, matches prescribed family compositions with competitive generation quality.
- Selected templates raise predicted stable/metastable fractions without reinforcement learning or extra optimization.

### [Pandora's Toolbox Or Why Agent Safety Cannot Be Verified](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25325v1)
**Authors:** Mario Franco, Carlos Gershenson
**Published:** 2026-11-19
**Summary:** Coupling a fixed language model with a general computer makes agent safety undecidable, and even under finite bounds it is PSPACE-complete and non-compositional.

**What's new**
- Proves no algorithm can predict whether an agent will run a semantically equivalent dangerous program.
- Shows guardrails, blacklists, whitelists, and certifications can only yield imperfect safety.

- Discusses relaxed notions of safety that could be used in practice.

### [Altermagnetic magnon splitting in hematite: from bulk g-wave to planar i-wave](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25284v1)

**Published:** 2026-11-18
**Summary:** Ab initio study of hematite magnons reveals g-wave bulk altermagnetic splitting plus a planar i-wave component at the Brillouin zone boundary.

**What's new**
- Fully relativistic ab initio magnon calculations quantify anisotropic and altermagnetic band splitting and lifetimes in hematite.
- Dipolar interactions cause sizable non-altermagnetic splitting and opposite-chirality magnon hybridization, reducing spin expectation near Gamma.
